## 1. Impeccable's structure: first-party, generated, why Rust, what the JSONL is

### Takeaway
The real source is much smaller than 4469 files: it is `skill/` (prompts), `crates/` (the Rust engine), `scripts/`, `browser-bundle/`, `cli/`, `extension/`, `ui/`, and `tests/`. Rust is not vendored code but the main runtime: one static binary, a port of the earlier Node engine. The 78 JSONL files are not an eval but frozen parity vectors of Rust against JS, and they say nothing about design quality.

## 3. The detector: inventory, technique, tests, precision/recall, justification of bans

### Takeaway
62 deterministic rules (31 slop, 31 quality) on four engines: regex over sources, static HTML with its own cascade, a browser through CDP, and pixel contrast. Test coverage for false positives is serious, and rules are really corrected from the markup of live sites. But there is no summary precision/recall, the raw corpus is closed, and the repository has no data that a pattern is more frequent in AI output than in human work.

## 4. Context files, brand, modes, and enforced variation

### Takeaway
`PRODUCT.md` holds product truth and deliberately contains no look; `DESIGN.md` is tokens in YAML plus prose in the Stitch format, and the extraction is done by the model itself from an instruction. In v4 the brand/product registers were replaced by four modes at the surface level. Variation is created by a die rolled over the model's ranked list and a closed catalog, but the brief and the existing world override it.

### Inferences
- [inference] By its nature, anti-slop assesses a result relative to all sites, while a brand book assesses it relative to one source. The conflict is not cosmetic: a cream brand color will get a finding that counts in the exit code, and only a manual `ignore` will fix it.
- [inference] A ban with an enumeration works as a temporary shift of the distribution: the model moves to a neighboring peak, and the list grows. For a brand, a positive binding to tokens is more durable: what is declared is allowed.

## 8. The family: mechanism, determinism, brand, evidence

### Takeaway
Only Impeccable and taste-skill have real browser checkers, but both measure compliance with their own taste, not with the client's brand. ui-ux-pro-max is the only one that checks search quality on a held-out sample and the only one with a simple brand flow, but its brand check is weak. Anthropic, Emil, and Vercel are pure prose. No repository has a controlled with-and-without comparison.

## What to take
- **Tokens as the norm, prose as context.** YAML tokens plus Named Rules, a schema version in a comment, a doctor for drift, with drift of truth honestly marked as a proxy — [document.md#L3](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/c3bb418ee38ee49a6b999b7752c1368f3123a2bd/skill/reference/document.md#L3), [CLAUDE.md#L66-L75](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/c3bb418ee38ee49a6b999b7752c1368f3123a2bd/CLAUDE.md#L66-L75).
- **Two hook levels** with deduplication and honest attribution of old violations: this removes attempts to fix someone else's debt — [stop_baseline.rs#L1-L18](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/c3bb418ee38ee49a6b999b7752c1368f3123a2bd/crates/hook/src/stop_baseline.rs#L1-L18).
- **Rule packs.** Your own rules in the namespace `mypack/...` without a fork; the engine is Apache-2.0 and compiles to wasm. There is already a first external consumer — [docs/ENGINE.md#L150-L243](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/c3bb418ee38ee49a6b999b7752c1368f3123a2bd/docs/ENGINE.md#L150-L243). The question of build or buy must be decided first; `publish = false` in the crates means a git dependency.
- **Three detection layers with one logic:** text, a static cascade, a browser behind the shared `Dom` trait.
- **The rule method:** should-flag/should-pass, a minimum of false-positive forms, a millisecond budget per edit, marking "pattern precision" and "harm" on real sites with demotion and removal of rules.
- **A gate as numbers, not conviction.** `comp-diff` with verdicts per region and the hint "one CSS edit" transfers to "build against the brand reference." Calibration against human pins is especially valuable — [COMP-FIDELITY.md#L194-L205](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/c3bb418ee38ee49a6b999b7752c1368f3123a2bd/docs/COMP-FIDELITY.md#L194-L205).
- **The procedure in the tool's output and in state on disk**, not in prose (`NEXT` lines, `state.json`).
- **A separate reviewer without the build history.**
- **An activation eval with neighbors** and a cap on the description length to fit the Claude Code listing.
- **Modes at the surface level:** on a working screen the brand is limited to font, palette, density, and one move. A ready formula for a brand book.
- **Parsing the `DESIGN.md` prose with negations taken into account** (`.eyebrow` is allowed unless condemned).
- **From the others:** `MASTER.md` with per-page overrides and an evaluator with held-out (ui-ux-pro-max); FAIL/WARN with a crop and a report of what could not be checked (taste-skill); `file:line` output and MUST/SHOULD/NEVER (Vercel); subject standards with escalation (Emil); compactness of 9 KB and "the brief wins" (Anthropic).

## What to avoid
- **Inconsistent severity.** A brand-drift rule at the urgent level, but with advisory and a default filter. For a brand book, token violations must be visible at once, and strictness must be set explicitly.
- **Taste rules that do not know about the brand.** If a token is declared, it should automatically lift a taste rule that contradicts it, as now with purple, but for all rules.
- **Enumerated bans and recommended font lists.** They create the next wave and endless catalog updates.
- **Self-service of exceptions by the agent without an audit.** Exceptions must be visible in the PR with the author and the reason.
- **Relying on the agent to run the gate itself.** An external point is needed: CI and/or a hook that does not depend on the model's discipline.
- **Growth of prose** as a reaction to failures. The authors themselves replaced prose with measurement.
- **Hard-coded taste references** (figma.com, x.ai, the ban on violet) that are incompatible with someone else's brand.
- **Runtime loading of instructions without version pinning** (Vercel) and variation that depends on a closed service.
- **Unconfirmed claims.** "Proven" and "measured" without published data will not convince a reviewer. Closed evals cannot be checked.
- **Regex-based brand checks** on literals as the only level: `var()` and Tailwind classes must be resolved to values.
- **Catalogs with empty justification** (161 of 192 rows without Reasoning).

## Open questions
- **Does any skill give a measurable improvement over having no skill?** There is no public answer. A minimal design for our own eval: N briefs × with/without × several model families, blind human rating, n≥3, diversity by fonts and colors plus a brand-fidelity metric.
- **Do anti-slop bans create new convergence?** Indirect signs are strong, but there is no measurement. We need to count the distribution of fonts, colors, and layouts with and without the skill on identical briefs.
- **Is the advisory filter for `design-system-*` a deliberate decision or an oversight?** Confirmed statically; it is worth checking by running the binary on a project with `DESIGN.md` and possibly asking the author.
- **How do `design-system-*` work on projects with Tailwind and CSS variables, where there are almost no literals?** A value with `var(` is skipped; the real coverage is unknown without a run.
- **What are the detector's overall precision and recall?** There are only scattered numbers per rule, and the corpus and judges are closed.
- **Is it worth building on the Impeccable engine?** A rule pack removes most of the work, but we need to check API stability, the time gap between versions, and the license consequences for the catalog (the catalog is closed, the engine is open).
- **Do the approaches apply to native and non-web media?** The detector is web-only; a brand book is broader (presentations, email, social media). The projects studied have no answer.
