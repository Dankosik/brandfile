# Brand voice, second pass: Russian data and guides, informality in a failure, whether voice distinguishes brands

Scope of work: 39 WebSearch queries out of the 50 allowed, about 100 tool calls against a target of 80. All seven questions were covered, but unevenly. What is unfinished is listed in the Gaps.

Access labels. `text`: I read the text layer of an opened file received through WebFetch myself (not the whole document, and I state which parts). `abstract`: the same, but the abstract only. `description`: a publisher page or a catalog. `digest`: a summary by an auxiliary model from WebSearch or WebFetch, even if it looks like a page. Digests make mistakes: one of them asserted a norm «вы вошла» ["you [polite/plural] came in", with a feminine singular verb], which contradicts academic grammar (see question 3), so numbers from digests must be checked.

A limit of the environment that matters for every negative conclusion. The eLibrary and CyberLeninka databases were not queried as databases: the search ran through general web search (which works from the US), and search forms were forbidden by the task. From our environment, the connection was refused by journals.rcsi.science, psy-journal.hse.ru, moloprom.kemsu.ru, philology-journal.ru, gramota.net, and rusgram.ru. For gramota.ru there was first one response, then 429 and a refusal. allo.tochka.com gave a certificate error, sberbank.com dropped the connection twice, library.kab.ac.ug gave 504 twice, and hub.hku.hk, mdpi.com, and the Taylor & Francis mirror gave 403. "Not found" below means "not found in the listed queries and accessible hosts".

## 1. Russian experiments on a company's style: ты/вы, register, infostyle, chatbots

### Takeaway
I found no controlled experiment on «ты» and «вы» ["you" informal and "you" formal/plural] in a company's voice, on the formal and informal register of a brand, or on инфостиль [infostyle, a Russian plain-style editing school] on Russian material in the nine target queries. The only Russian controlled experiment on the style of an organization's text that I found is read directly, but it is about Bank of Russia press releases and students' inflation expectations, not about a brand and trust. There is a nearest substitute for «ты/вы»: T/V experiments in French, German, and Dutch, and there is no universal winner in them.

### Cited Findings

**Direct Russian evidence on style**

- [EMPIRICAL] Kartaev, Klachkova (2025), Вестник Московского университета. Серия 6. Экономика [Moscow University Bulletin. Series 6. Economics], vol. 60, doi 10.55959/MSU0130-0105-6-60-3-2. A laboratory experiment, 274 students (economists and non-economists), control and experimental groups. Original press releases against ones simplified by a generative model; the simplification was checked with the ARI index. In the rate-increase scenario, the simplified text lowered inflation expectations by about 1.8–2 percentage points, and in the rate-cut scenario the effect is not significant. Expectations were measured, not trust. Access: `text` (abstract, data, results, conclusion). — [PDF](https://msuecon.elpub.ru/jour/article/download/1562/636)
- [TRANSFER] Only one thing carries over from this work: in Russian, the simplicity of a text can shift the reaction, and the effect depends on the content of the news and is asymmetric. What does not carry over: a different sender (a regulator), a different outcome, students only, and the simplification mixes dozens of features, so the effect of a single style rule is not isolated.
- [PRIMARY-SOURCE FACT] Kartaev and Klachkova refer to the ARI index built for modern Russian texts and to 44 text characteristics from an article by Evstigneeva and Sidorovsky (2021). I did not read the article itself. Access: `text` (a retelling inside Kartaev's work). — [PDF](https://msuecon.elpub.ru/jour/article/download/1562/636); a description of the work on the [Bank of Russia site](https://www.cbr.ru/press/event/?id=12252) (`digest`).

**Transfer from other languages that distinguish T/V** (the grammatical category is the same, the social meanings may differ)

- [TRANSFER] Ollier, Nißen, von Wangenheim (2022), Frontiers in Public Health. A four-way interaction of T/V, language, age, and gender. Among French speakers, "tu" won with young women and older men, and "vous" with young men and older women. ### Inferences
- [RESEARCHER INFERENCE] The report's phrase "almost nothing found" is confirmed on a larger number of queries, but only for the open web. It does not mean that eLibrary has no suitable articles or dissertations.
- [RESEARCHER INFERENCE] The support for «ты/вы» now looks different. Stated preferences (Anketolog) are supplemented by experiments in neighboring languages. They show an interaction with age, gender, and brand image, and show no separate main effect. In the German part of Ollier, the V form gives a smaller spread of ratings, so «вы» can be defended as the lower-risk option, not as the more pleasant one. This is a transfer across languages, and it must be tested on your own audience.

## 3. Russian interface language: gender, address, declension of brands

### Takeaway
The report's table can now be partly checked. Gramota's reference service confirms the declension of a name without a generic word. Academic grammar confirms the plural verb with the polite «Вы» ["You"], so the report is wrong when it says in general that the past tense shows gender: with «вы» it does not, with «ты» and «я» it does. A lowercase «вы» does not follow from the reference works: they consider the capital correct for addressing one person and leave the choice to the author. I found no authoritative guidance on gender-neutral wording in interfaces.

### Cited Findings

- [PRIMARY-SOURCE FACT] Gramota's reference service, question No. 334065 (answer of 26 August 2026): with a generic word the name is not declined; without a generic word it takes the place of a declinable noun and is declined, «в "Метрополе"» ["in the Metropol"]. The question of whether failing to decline brand names is an error or an editorial policy is not directly settled. Access: `digest`. — [Gramota](https://gramota.ru/spravka/vopros/334065)
- [PRIMARY-SOURCE FACT] The same site, in another answer (about the name «Страна Девелопмент» [Strana Development]): by the norm, without a generic word it should be declined, but sometimes declension creates legal difficulties. Access: `digest`. — [a selection of Gramota answers](http://new.gramota.ru/spravka/buro/search-answer?s=%D0%9D%D0%B0%D0%B7%D0%B2%D0%B0%D0%BD%D0%B8%D0%B5+%D0%B2+%D0%BA%D0%B0%D0%B2%D1%8B%D1%87%D0%BA%D0%B0%D1%85+%D0%BD%D0%B5+%D1%81%D0%BA%D0%BB%D0%BE%D0%BD%D1%8F%D0%B5%D1%82%D1%81%D1%8F)

There are no direct studies or reference guidance on interfaces.

### Inferences
- [RESEARCHER INFERENCE] The price of choosing «ты» is higher than the report holds: in the «ты» mode the past tense reveals gender («ты вошёл», «ты вошла» ["you came in", masculine and feminine]), and this can be neutralized only by rewriting. In the «вы» mode the verb does not show gender, and what remains are nouns about the person and possibly adjectives. Our example: «Вы вошли» ["You came in"] is safe, while «Вы наш новый клиент» ["You are our new client", masculine noun] already assigns gender.
- [RESEARCHER INFERENCE] The same with the speaker: «я» ["I"] in the past tense requires a gender for the agent or bot («я проверил», «я проверила» ["I checked", masculine and feminine]), while «мы» ["we"] does not. English works on "I" do not have this problem. For example, Crolic specifically picks the gender-neutral Jamie. In Russian, such neutrality is impossible with «я» in the past tense.
- [RESEARCHER INFERENCE] The infinitive on buttons removes the «ты/вы» choice where the English rule speaks of the imperative. So a linter for consistency of address should look at messages and hints, not at buttons.
- [RESEARCHER INFERENCE] A generic word («в сервисе "Икс"» ["in the service 'X'"]) is a practical way to avoid declension in a template, if the company's brand cannot be declined or its form is not defined. This is a recipe, not a norm.

### Gaps
- Full adjectives with «Вы» need to be read up in Russian Grammar or in Rosenthal.
- Rules for declining Latin-script names in substitutions were not found; Gramota speaks only about quotation marks.
- "Number substitution" (agreement with numerals) was not checked against reference works.

## 4. Informality in a service failure: what is actually established

### Takeaway
The most defensible wording is this: there is no dispute as such, the works measure different things. Emojis lower the rating of competence, and the abstract of Li and coauthors does not report what exactly changes in a failure.
