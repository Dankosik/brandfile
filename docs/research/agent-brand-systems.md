# How to make agents follow a brand book

Date: November 6, 2026.
Status: code read at pinned SHAs, nothing from the repositories was run; the architecture, the rollout process, and the thresholds are our inference, not tested in practice.

## Conclusions: compile the brand book into checks, not into one more file to read

**Recommendation.** Build a file-based brand compiler with skills on top of it (option B in the architecture section). Values live in DTCG 2025.10. Voice, logo, imagery, composition, and rules tied to a check live in our own `brand.yaml` schema; people's decisions live in a log. `DESIGN.md`, a short `BRAND.md`, CSS, Tailwind, and linter configs are only generated. One command, `brand check`, gives one verdict; agent hooks only call it, and CI is the safety net where the agent cannot fix anything. Skills remain for the heavy procedures: create a brand, roll it out into a live product, migrate, run a review.

**What the review showed.** None of the projects we reviewed has independent controlled evidence that it improves the result; stars say nothing about effectiveness. The strong parts are scattered: the creation process in GoldLegendW80/brand, the reading format in DESIGN.md, the value layer in DTCG and Terrazzo, the render check and fingerprinted approval in brandsystem-mcp, hooks and rule calibration in Impeccable. Among the projects reviewed, no one has assembled the whole loop (brand, compilation into rules, feedback that does not depend on the agent's decision, blocking, a check of the code agents actually write), although segments of it are closed (see "What exactly is closed").

**Why the design looks the same.** It is mostly not a token problem. Color and font are the most visible, structure is the largest unfixed remainder, and thin context and selection during training sit further up the chain. Confidence is medium: no one has run a direct "tokens versus structure" experiment. A skill that ships only tokens will probably produce the same landing page in new colors. Banning the tells moves the mode instead of removing it. A brand does not need diverse generation; it needs its own mode: far from other brands, a stable identity, structure that changes from task to task.

**A company with a product.** First capture, then decide, then freeze, and only then change the visuals. An extractor supplies evidence, not canon: the best primary-color hit rate we found is 71% on 17 sites. Canon arises only from a recorded decision by a named person. What we consider new in our proposal is the pairing: a value's status sets how strict the check is, and the `protected` status overrides anti-slop.

**How to measure.** Compliance and distinctiveness are different questions, and they must not be folded into one score. A pilot on 6 brands catches failures and tunes the tool, but confirms nothing; the claim "better than existing solutions" is allowed only after 8–12 brands and a comparison with active competitors. Getting to the decision on whether to continue takes on the order of $2–8k, 2–3 person-weeks, and 3–4 calendar weeks (our estimate).

**Cyrillic.** Our team works in Russian-language e-commerce, and the tools we reviewed, judging by their code, break silently on Russian. This is a hard requirement and a possible differentiator; not confirmed by a run.

**Where to start.** First the verifier and baseline measurements without the skill, then the schema and the compiler, then `brand check` with a hook and CI. Creating a brand from scratch comes last among the skills: its value cannot be judged without the check further down the chain, and agent-generated instructions can be worse than no instructions.

### How to read the labels

A label appears as the link text where the strength of a claim matters; our own inferences usually have no link.

| Label | What it means |
|---|---|
| `code` | Read in the code at a pinned SHA. Behavior inferred from reading; nothing was run |
| `README` | Claimed in the project's documentation, not confirmed by code |
| `test` | The repository has a test or eval; we did not run it |
| `study` | Measured in a published study, with caveats alongside; "abstract" means only the abstract was read |
| `vendor` | Self-report by a party with a stake in the result |
| `inference` | Our inference: the basis is named, and the inference can be disputed |

## What already exists: each has its own strong part, none has the whole

### GoldLegendW80/brand: a strong process before generation and zero enforcement after

**What works.** The best methodology we found against generic output sits before generation: discovery raw material with a core tension and a non-obvious true trait ([code](https://github.com/GoldLegendW80/brand/blob/e2f2f4de9f2166c744a6079ce65fbf0c14e34db9/skills/make-brand/SKILL.md#L144-L147)), five gates with an audit and a break from the category's codes ([code](https://github.com/GoldLegendW80/brand/blob/e2f2f4de9f2166c744a6079ce65fbf0c14e34db9/skills/make-brand/assets/craft/CRAFT.md#L13-L43)), four required rationale fields per direction ([code](https://github.com/GoldLegendW80/brand/blob/e2f2f4de9f2166c744a6079ce65fbf0c14e34db9/skills/make-brand/SKILL.md#L219-L227)). Two things are done well: the split between creation and consumption with a single manifest ([README](https://github.com/GoldLegendW80/brand/blob/e2f2f4de9f2166c744a6079ce65fbf0c14e34db9/README.md#L95-L100)) and an operational voice: We Are / We Are Not, a tone grid, before-and-after pairs ([code](https://github.com/GoldLegendW80/brand/blob/e2f2f4de9f2166c744a6079ce65fbf0c14e34db9/skills/make-brand/assets/editorial/03-VOICE-STORYTELLING.tmpl.md#L13-L58)). "Render-safe" in the code means a deterministic offline render ([code](https://github.com/GoldLegendW80/brand/blob/e2f2f4de9f2166c744a6079ce65fbf0c14e34db9/skills/make-brand/SKILL.md#L391-L396)), that is, the file opens the same way. This does not ensure brand compliance.

**Where it is weak.** There is no formal schema. The direction lint only warns ([code](https://github.com/GoldLegendW80/brand/blob/e2f2f4de9f2166c744a6079ce65fbf0c14e34db9/skills/make-brand/scripts/build-directions.mjs#L16)), the conformance lint exists only as instruction text ([code](https://github.com/GoldLegendW80/brand/blob/e2f2f4de9f2166c744a6079ce65fbf0c14e34db9/skills/use-brand/SKILL.md#L186-L198)), the loader drops `contrast` and `neverPair` ([code](https://github.com/GoldLegendW80/brand/blob/e2f2f4de9f2166c744a6079ce65fbf0c14e34db9/skills/use-brand/assets/_brand.mjs#L54-L66)) and silently substitutes a default palette ([code](https://github.com/GoldLegendW80/brand/blob/e2f2f4de9f2166c744a6079ce65fbf0c14e34db9/skills/use-brand/assets/_brand.mjs#L59-L63)). The project does not reach product code, commits, or CI: an agent writing a React component gets `tokens.css`, and nothing stops it from typing in a hex. Brownfield comes down to crawling a public portal, and neutral colors are discarded ([code](https://github.com/GoldLegendW80/brand/blob/e2f2f4de9f2166c744a6079ce65fbf0c14e34db9/skills/make-brand/scripts/fetch-brand.mjs#L71-L76)). Reading found runtime-dependent defects: the logo does not make it into PPTX ([code](https://github.com/GoldLegendW80/brand/blob/e2f2f4de9f2166c744a6079ce65fbf0c14e34db9/skills/use-brand/scripts/deck.mjs#L209-L212)), some logos are invisible in PDF ([code](https://github.com/GoldLegendW80/brand/blob/e2f2f4de9f2166c744a6079ce65fbf0c14e34db9/skills/use-brand/scripts/pdf.mjs#L123-L128)), the video brief fails the public validator ([code](https://github.com/GoldLegendW80/llm-video-maker/blob/cee12add24317c50f8a9f9de93749089a59d9f99/skills/make-video/scripts/validate-brief.mjs#L69-L71)). Only a run can confirm them. There are no tests, no CI, and no output examples.

**Why** ([inference](https://github.com/GoldLegendW80/brand/commit/e2f2f4de9f2166c744a6079ce65fbf0c14e34db9)): effectively a single author, and the last commit is 3354 lines across 51 files, moving the video pipeline onto shared branding; the bet is that the LLM will carry out the prose on its own. Take the ideas, not the dependency. Project summary: 0 stars, 7 installs on [skills.sh](https://www.skills.sh/GoldLegendW80/brand/make-brand).

### google-labs-code/design.md: a good reading format, but not a contract

**What works.** YAML tokens plus prose ([README](https://github.com/google-labs-code/design.md/blob/1ed38aa9dd4451b9039f9aeb9ed1d2579edafddf/docs/spec.md#L17-L22)) are cheap for an agent: on the repository's example, the front matter costs about 1030 tokens versus about 4235 for the [JSON from the same example](https://github.com/google-labs-code/design.md/blob/1ed38aa9dd4451b9039f9aeb9ed1d2579edafddf/examples/paws-and-paths/design_tokens.json#L586-L589) for the same 71 tokens (chars/4 estimate; the shape of this JSON does not match the current exporter). The 11-rule linter outputs JSON ([code](https://github.com/google-labs-code/design.md/blob/1ed38aa9dd4451b9039f9aeb9ed1d2579edafddf/packages/cli/src/linter/linter/rules/index.ts#L31-L43)), and the `omitted` field separates a decision from forgetfulness ([README](https://github.com/google-labs-code/design.md/blob/1ed38aa9dd4451b9039f9aeb9ed1d2579edafddf/docs/spec.md#L127-L136)). Demand is large: 1.37 million downloads in 30 days ([npm](https://api.npmjs.org/downloads/point/last-month/@google%2fdesign.md)), but that is a signal of demand, not of effect.

**Where it is weak.** The linter reads only the file itself; nothing checks the generated code: [issue #181](https://github.com/google-labs-code/design.md/issues/181) about checking the render against the tokens has no answer, and [PR #80](https://github.com/google-labs-code/design.md/pull/80) with an HTML preview was rejected. Per the code, unreadable YAML turns into a warning with `errors: 0` ([code](https://github.com/google-labs-code/design.md/blob/1ed38aa9dd4451b9039f9aeb9ed1d2579edafddf/packages/cli/src/linter/lint.ts#L64-L85)), so the exit code cannot be treated as a gate; not confirmed by a run. A clean lint has already been presented as WCAG AA compliance with pairs below 4.5:1 in the CSS ([#116](https://github.com/google-labs-code/design.md/issues/116)). The format has no logo, imagery, voice, modes, or shadows. The DTCG export drops components ([code](https://github.com/google-labs-code/design.md/blob/1ed38aa9dd4451b9039f9aeb9ed1d2579edafddf/packages/cli/src/linter/dtcg/handler.ts#L41-L51)), resolves aliases, and loses the prose, and there is no import at all, although the spec promises easy conversion in both directions ([code](https://github.com/google-labs-code/design.md/blob/1ed38aa9dd4451b9039f9aeb9ed1d2579edafddf/docs/spec.md#L15)). Real files are large: about 29 KB on average, roughly 7300 tokens by estimate ([code](https://github.com/VoltAgent/awesome-design-md/tree/fcc70284932276d085f01ef9f83edde90b1258cb/design-md)). At Atlassian, an 80-kilobyte file cost 7.21 million input tokens versus 3.75 million for MCP and recreated components more often; the number of runs is not stated ([vendor](https://www.atlassian.com/blog/how-we-build/atlassians-design-md-is-here-what-we-learned-testing-portable-design-context-in-practice)).

**Why.** It is a deliberate position: values in the file count as context, not as render instructions ([README](https://github.com/google-labs-code/design.md/blob/1ed38aa9dd4451b9039f9aeb9ed1d2579edafddf/PHILOSOPHY.md#L42)), the maintainer turns down new token types ([#74](https://github.com/google-labs-code/design.md/pull/74#issuecomment-4712517667)), the status is alpha ([code](https://github.com/google-labs-code/design.md/blob/1ed38aa9dd4451b9039f9aeb9ed1d2579edafddf/packages/cli/src/linter/spec-config.yaml#L19)). The slogan about normative tokens conflicts with this position, and the format cannot be relied on as a contract ([inference]). It pays to generate it as a reading view: the cost is low because the file is derived.

### DTCG with its toolchain: the best value layer, and only values

**What works.** Version 2025.10 is stable, and there is nothing newer; it is a Final Community Group Report, not a W3C Standard ([code](https://github.com/design-tokens/community-group/blob/882ebd6716abef9a46d8ef5fb12e82009d6cee2e/www/src/pages/TR/2025.10/format/index.html#L237), [announcement](https://www.w3.org/community/design-tokens/2025/10/28/design-tokens-specification-reaches-first-stable-version/)). It has 13 types ([code](https://github.com/design-tokens/community-group/blob/882ebd6716abef9a46d8ef5fb12e82009d6cee2e/schemas/src/2025.10/format/tokenType.json)), aliases, `$deprecated` ([code](https://github.com/design-tokens/community-group/blob/882ebd6716abef9a46d8ef5fb12e82009d6cee2e/technical-reports/format/design-token.md#L194)), and modes through the Resolver module ([code](https://github.com/design-tokens/community-group/blob/882ebd6716abef9a46d8ef5fb12e82009d6cee2e/technical-reports/resolver/syntax.md#L5-L15)). Of the tools checked, Terrazzo implements Resolver and contains a real 27-rule linter ([code](https://github.com/terrazzoapp/terrazzo/blob/519ee07b2f947b23cd1279264bececdabd7712b8/packages/parser/src/lint/plugin-core/index.ts#L56-L84)), though contrast, descriptions, and modes are switched on by hand ([code](https://github.com/terrazzoapp/terrazzo/blob/519ee07b2f947b23cd1279264bececdabd7712b8/packages/parser/src/lint/plugin-core/index.ts#L95-L113)). Style Dictionary is the most widely used (9.16 million downloads, [npm](https://api.npmjs.org/downloads/point/last-month/style-dictionary)), but supports 2025.10 only partly ([README](https://github.com/style-dictionary/style-dictionary/blob/795233d9bee6a7b5787d685d75d626df3399d24d/docs/src/content/docs/info/DTCG.mdx#L13-L16)), and a search found no Resolver in it.

**Where it is weak.** The spec has no voice, logo, layout, or usage rules, and roles are not expressed in machine form: groups are arbitrary, and tools must not infer purpose from them ([code](https://github.com/design-tokens/community-group/blob/882ebd6716abef9a46d8ef5fb12e82009d6cee2e/technical-reports/format/terminology.md#L99)). Putting critical data in `$extensions` is not recommended ([code](https://github.com/design-tokens/community-group/blob/882ebd6716abef9a46d8ef5fb12e82009d6cee2e/technical-reports/format/design-token.md#L184-L190)). The JSON Schema has holes ([#439](https://github.com/design-tokens/community-group/issues/439), [#448](https://github.com/design-tokens/community-group/issues/448)). Figma imports only flat types ([docs](https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/articles/15343816063383-Modes-for-variables)). Claimed compatibility is often false: GoldLegend ([code](https://github.com/GoldLegendW80/brand/blob/e2f2f4de9f2166c744a6079ce65fbf0c14e34db9/skills/use-brand/scripts/tokens.mjs#L115)), brandsystem-mcp ([code](https://github.com/Brandcode-Studio/brandsystem-mcp/blob/eab3927a9eee5c6bada016ccf1c0bf007af696fb/src/lib/dtcg-compiler.ts#L140-L143)), and the DESIGN.md export write values in forms that 2025.10 does not allow. We did not run that output through validators ourselves. Terrazzo's rules take only tokens as input; application code is invisible to them ([inference]).

**Why.** That is the boundary of the specification: typed values, not a brand book. For a value layer, this is a plus.

### brandsystem-mcp: the best checking ideas, which the agent is free not to call

**What works.** The pipeline is deterministic, with no LLM on the server ([code](https://github.com/Brandcode-Studio/brandsystem-mcp/blob/eab3927a9eee5c6bada016ccf1c0bf007af696fb/package.json#L31-L42)). The strongest part is `brand_preflight render=true`: computed styles in Chrome with the network closed, CIEDE2000 with thresholds ([code](https://github.com/Brandcode-Studio/brandsystem-mcp/blob/eab3927a9eee5c6bada016ccf1c0bf007af696fb/src/lib/rendered-compliance.ts#L118-L154)), the font actually rendered ([code](https://github.com/Brandcode-Studio/brandsystem-mcp/blob/eab3927a9eee5c6bada016ccf1c0bf007af696fb/src/lib/rendered-compliance.ts#L225-L235)). Approval is tied to the sha256 of the sources and is silently downgraded on any change ([code](https://github.com/Brandcode-Studio/brandsystem-mcp/blob/eab3927a9eee5c6bada016ccf1c0bf007af696fb/src/lib/approval-state.ts#L137-L167)). The methodology is unusually honest: the authors withdrew the published 5/5 when the checker was running idle on zero rules, and got 1/5 ([test](https://github.com/Brandcode-Studio/brandsystem-mcp/blob/eab3927a9eee5c6bada016ccf1c0bf007af696fb/eval/RESULTS.md#L128-L158)).

**Where it is weak.** Every check is called at the agent's discretion. There are no hooks and no CI command ([code](https://github.com/Brandcode-Studio/brandsystem-mcp/blob/eab3927a9eee5c6bada016ccf1c0bf007af696fb/src/cli.ts#L41-L70)); enforcement is text in the instructions ([code](https://github.com/Brandcode-Studio/brandsystem-mcp/blob/eab3927a9eee5c6bada016ccf1c0bf007af696fb/src/lib/native-exports.ts#L560-L565)). Four engines with different palettes and metrics give different verdicts on the same content ([inference]). The static engines do not see Tailwind classes, CSS-in-JS, or `<link>` ([code](https://github.com/Brandcode-Studio/brandsystem-mcp/blob/eab3927a9eee5c6bada016ccf1c0bf007af696fb/src/tools/brand-check-compliance.ts#L165-L169)), and preflight does not accept a dev-server URL ([code](https://github.com/Brandcode-Studio/brandsystem-mcp/blob/eab3927a9eee5c6bada016ccf1c0bf007af696fb/src/tools/brand-preflight.ts#L982-L990)). With zero rules, the check passes and recommends publishing ([code](https://github.com/Brandcode-Studio/brandsystem-mcp/blob/eab3927a9eee5c6bada016ccf1c0bf007af696fb/src/tools/brand-check-compliance.ts#L240-L253)). The default profile of 12 tools has no extraction from PDF or Figma and no voice authoring ([code](https://github.com/Brandcode-Studio/brandsystem-mcp/blob/eab3927a9eee5c6bada016ccf1c0bf007af696fb/src/lib/tool-profile.ts#L21-L47)), so the text check after an automatic run from a URL is empty ([inference]). PDF comes down to searching for `#hex` in the text layer ([code](https://github.com/Brandcode-Studio/brandsystem-mcp/blob/eab3927a9eee5c6bada016ccf1c0bf007af696fb/src/lib/pdf-extractor.ts#L50-L99)). Source rank outweighs confidence ([code](https://github.com/Brandcode-Studio/brandsystem-mcp/blob/eab3927a9eee5c6bada016ccf1c0bf007af696fb/src/lib/confidence.ts#L86-L93)): it follows that a named `--brand-primary` can lose to the first colored button background, but we did not reproduce this at runtime. Effect: 33–40% of tasks completed, on one model, five synthetic tasks, and its own checker, with no "without the tool" arm ([test](https://github.com/Brandcode-Studio/brandsystem-mcp/blob/eab3927a9eee5c6bada016ccf1c0bf007af696fb/eval/RESULTS.md#L219-L229)), against a promise of on-brand on the first try ([README](https://github.com/Brandcode-Studio/brandsystem-mcp/blob/eab3927a9eee5c6bada016ccf1c0bf007af696fb/README.md#L48)).

**Why** ([inference]): the breadth is there in the catalog of 44 tools, but not on the first-run path; the package works as a funnel into an agency's service, hence the focus on governance rather than developer convenience. The context cost is about 3.35k tokens in core and 10.1k in full, without the JSON Schema (chars/4 estimate). The project is young: 15 stars and 1162 downloads a month ([npm](https://api.npmjs.org/downloads/point/last-month/@brandsystem/mcp)) say nothing about effect.

### Impeccable: the most mature loop, but a loop for taste, not for a brand

**What works.** 62 rules on four engines: regex, static HTML with the cascade, a browser, pixel contrast ([code](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/c3bb418ee38ee49a6b999b7752c1368f3123a2bd/crates/foundation/src/registry.rs#L685-L690)). A hook after each edit plus a deep pass on Stop ([code](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/c3bb418ee38ee49a6b999b7752c1368f3123a2bd/skill/reference/hooks.md#L5-L7)), honest attribution of old violations ([code](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/c3bb418ee38ee49a6b999b7752c1368f3123a2bd/crates/hook/src/stop_baseline.rs#L1-L18)), `detect` with exit code 2 for CI ([README](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/c3bb418ee38ee49a6b999b7752c1368f3123a2bd/README.md#L78-L90)). The most useful methodological finding is the split between "the pattern was found correctly" and "the pattern does harm": `clipped-overflow-container`, with pattern precision 0.08 and harm 0.04, was downgraded to advisory ([test](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/c3bb418ee38ee49a6b999b7752c1368f3123a2bd/tests/oracle/DELTAS.md#L543-L548)). An established product world is inherited, and the mode is set at the surface level ([code](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/c3bb418ee38ee49a6b999b7752c1368f3123a2bd/skill/reference/new-work.md#L9-L16), [code](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/c3bb418ee38ee49a6b999b7752c1368f3123a2bd/skill/SKILL.src.md#L37-L44)). The engine is open, and you can add your own rules as packages ([code](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/c3bb418ee38ee49a6b999b7752c1368f3123a2bd/docs/ENGINE.md#L150-L243)).

**Where it is weak.** Brand drift is barely enforced by default: three of the four `design-system-*` rules are at the advisory level ([code](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/c3bb418ee38ee49a6b999b7752c1368f3123a2bd/crates/foundation/src/registry.rs#L574-L607)), and the hook drops such findings by default ([code](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/c3bb418ee38ee49a6b999b7752c1368f3123a2bd/crates/hook/src/hook_lib.rs#L415)), although the documentation promises the opposite ([README](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/c3bb418ee38ee49a6b999b7752c1368f3123a2bd/skill/reference/hooks.md#L7)). This conclusion comes from reading; the binary was not run. Only font, color with a tolerance of ±6 per channel, radius, and size are compared ([code](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/c3bb418ee38ee49a6b999b7752c1368f3123a2bd/crates/detect/src/design_system.rs#L1946-L2040)), and a value with `var(` counts as allowed ([code](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/c3bb418ee38ee49a6b999b7752c1368f3123a2bd/crates/detect/src/design_system.rs#L1569)). The hook always exits with code 0 ([code](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/c3bb418ee38ee49a6b999b7752c1368f3123a2bd/crates/hook/src/hook.rs#L2-L3)); `decision: block` exists only on Stop in Codex, Gemini, and Hermes ([code](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/c3bb418ee38ee49a6b999b7752c1368f3123a2bd/crates/hook/src/hook_lib.rs#L1820-L1851)). The agent saves exceptions itself ([code](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/c3bb418ee38ee49a6b999b7752c1368f3123a2bd/crates/hook/src/hook_lib.rs#L1306-L1320)), and after 6 edits to a file the hints stop ([code](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/c3bb418ee38ee49a6b999b7752c1368f3123a2bd/crates/hook/src/hook_lib.rs#L132)). The only token-based exception to a slop rule we found is a declared purple ([code](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/c3bb418ee38ee49a6b999b7752c1368f3123a2bd/crates/detect/src/design_system.rs#L770-L783)); in addition, the browser rules recognize classes named in the `DESIGN.md` prose ([code](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/c3bb418ee38ee49a6b999b7752c1368f3123a2bd/crates/detect/src/design_system.rs#L228-L269)). A brand's signature cream will get a finding that counts toward the exit code. The repository has no comparison of how often the patterns occur in AI work versus human work: no one has tested the claim "this gives away AI." The comparison with and without the skill sits in a private repository ([code](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/c3bb418ee38ee49a6b999b7752c1368f3123a2bd/CLAUDE.md#L386-L401)); there are no public numbers. Cost: a narrow edit is about 6k tokens, a new world from scratch about 35k (estimate), and the authors themselves recorded 183 KB of prose before the first write ([test](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/c3bb418ee38ee49a6b999b7752c1368f3123a2bd/docs/COMP-FIDELITY.md#L9-L15)). Models got around even the hard gates: the author admits that the floor there is set by the model's discipline ([test](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/c3bb418ee38ee49a6b999b7752c1368f3123a2bd/docs/COMP-FIDELITY.md#L179-L192)).

**Why** ([inference]): anti-slop judges the result against all sites, a brand book against one source. The conflict is not cosmetic. The project's notion of a brand book comes down to tokens and prose; there is no import of an external brand book.

### What exactly is closed

A grep across 13 repositories of brand skills and collections found no mentions of agent hooks or linter configs. It catches only specific words and did not cover Impeccable, taste-skill, or ui-ux-pro-max, so the conclusion "no one has built an end-to-end loop" does not follow from it. With these projects, the picture is as follows.

Let us split the loop into five links: the brand is created or extracted together with its non-token layer; the brand is compiled into rules; feedback arrives without the agent's decision; there is blocking or CI; the check sees TSX, Tailwind, or the render.

The **taste** loop is closed: Impeccable provides a hook and CI, taste-skill a browser check with exit 1, but both measure conformance to their own taste, not to the client's brand. The **values** loop against a token file is closed: dembrandt on the render, the vendor linters of Primer, Polaris, SLDS, and Atlassian in the code of their own systems; Lyse against the repository's scale. They do not read the brand book and know nothing about voice, logo, or usage rules. A loop for **adherence to a component library** is claimed by Lovable, and a loop for **brand rules on marketing content** by GenStudio: both are inside closed products, accuracy is not published, and they cannot be checked from outside. Closest to a brand in open code is the ui-ux-pro-max brand flow (`guidelines → tokens → CSS`, [code](https://github.com/nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill/blob/b7d5353e0130feb8c5574ef1251f8a1db1873ccb/.claude/skills/brand/scripts/sync-brand-to-tokens.cjs#L1-L20)), but its checks look at the file name, sizes, and literals, and there are no hooks in its settings ([code](https://github.com/nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill/blob/b7d5353e0130feb8c5574ef1251f8a1db1873ccb/stack/.claude/settings.json#L1-L14)).

**Not closed, among the projects reviewed:** a loop where one and the same open tool creates or extracts a brand with its non-token layer, compiles it into rules for future changes, returns violations to the agent regardless of its discipline, and backs this up in CI. The catalog is not exhaustive: skill registries hold thousands of entries, and we read the code only of the ones named. One more correction: carrying confidence over into check strictness is not entirely untouched ground. brandsystem-mcp makes color verdicts advisory until the primary is confirmed ([code](https://github.com/Brandcode-Studio/brandsystem-mcp/blob/eab3927a9eee5c6bada016ccf1c0bf007af696fb/src/lib/primary-uncertainty.ts#L24-L46)), but only for one field.

## Why agents converge: color is the most visible, but the remainder sits in structure

Convergence in UI has now been measured, though the evidence base is young: almost all of it is 2026 preprints, often with a conflict of interest and without a consistent human baseline. Homogenization is older than LLMs: the layout distance between sites fell by more than 30% back in 2003–2019 ([study, project page](https://cv-web-history.neocities.org/)). Agents inherited an already compressed distribution and compressed it further ([inference]).

The "does it close it" ratings below are our inference.

| Cause | Strength of evidence | What there is | Brand source of truth | What else is needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mode of the training corpus | Weak, indirect | A claim by Anthropic ([vendor](https://claude.com/blog/improving-frontend-design-through-skills)); no one has counted the corpus. Claude Code picks Tailwind in 68.4% and shadcn/ui in 90.1% ([study](https://amplifying.ai/research/claude-code-picks/report)), while on the public web Bootstrap is at 13.0% versus 0.3% for Tailwind ([W3Techs](https://w3techs.com/technologies/overview/css_framework)): the naive version does not hold | Partly: only on the specified axes | Coverage of all axes |
| Stack defaults in generators | Strong for the stack, medium for aesthetics | Bolt pins Tailwind, shadcn, Lucide ([vendor](https://github.com/stackblitz/starters/blob/878f97b57d3103548f59fcdbe0efa694b9aa6352/vite-shadcn/.bolt/prompt)); v0 was trained on shadcn defaults ([vendor](https://v0.app/docs/design-systems-legacy)) | Partly: the stack stays, the theme changes | Adapters for the real stack |

**Tokens or structure? Mostly structure, with medium confidence.** For structure: Kombai has 0.581 versus 0.902, but information architecture makes up 0.9 of the metric, and it deliberately ignores the theme ([study](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.11539v1)); the designers used Behance, and the authors sell a UI product. Our recount of Krebs's files: 76.9% of pages with two or more patterns have neither purple nor a stock font; 79.5% of pages with 4+ patterns have at least two structural ones; if, hypothetically, the whole surface layer were removed, 26.1% of all pages would still have two or more structural patterns ([data](https://github.com/AdrianKrebs/ai-design-checker)). Anthropic's skill evolved from font bans to structure as information and an ASCII wireframe ([vendor](https://github.com/anthropics/skills/blob/41bbe19d1a1a7eaab5e7bb9050a417e5c6cffc8f/skills/frontend-design/SKILL.md)).

For surface: the most frequent patterns in Krebs's data are surface ones (perma dark 34.7%, gradients 26.0%), and 61.5% of pages have at least one surface pattern versus 53.8% with a structural one. At Atlassian, the design file made the prototype recognizable, although the file contains more than colors ([vendor](https://www.atlassian.com/blog/how-we-build/atlassians-design-md-is-here-what-we-learned-testing-portable-design-context-in-practice)). The layer labeling in our recount is debatable: an accent stripe could count as structure, the detector catches only what is easy to check in the DOM, and the FAQ and the centered hero were the norm before LLMs too. The comparison of spreads in Design Theater cannot be used: the units are not comparable, and there is no significance test ([study](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.22928v2)). No one has run the decisive test: tokens, plus prose, plus a registry, plus page templates, with a structural-similarity metric. We have to run it.

**Second-order convergence: a ban moves the mode.** Anthropic writes that general instructions "shift the model to a different fixed palette rather than producing variety" ([vendor](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/prompt-engineering/prompting-claude-opus-4-8)). The timeline supports this: the skill grew from 583 words in December 2025 ([code](https://github.com/anthropics/skills/blob/00756142ab04c82a447693cf373c4e0c554d1005/skills/frontend-design/SKILL.md)) to 1516 in September 2026, and over that time the list of tells gained cream with a serif and terracotta, acid green on near-black, and stock chrome. Impeccable keeps a block of "new monoculture" fonts ([code](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/c3bb418ee38ee49a6b999b7752c1368f3123a2bd/crates/foundation/src/constants.rs#L85-L106)) and rules aimed at specific models ([code](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/c3bb418ee38ee49a6b999b7752c1368f3123a2bd/crates/foundation/src/registry.rs#L614-L650)). A micro-sample: with the skill, Playfair on warm paper and acid lime; three tasks and one run ([study, micro](https://github.com/dani-z/frontend-design-skill-benchmark)).

There is no multi-run measurement of frequencies with and without the ban. The cream style may be either a result of the bans or the model's base style: the Opus 4.8 documentation describes it as the default, while a closed Design Arena analysis calls Anthropic's style dark and purple ([vendor](https://x.com/DesignArena/status/2111884406868808191)). Neither can be passed off as a refutation of the other.

**What follows for the skill** ([inference]). A brand book must contain decisions, not descriptions: not "we are bold," but what goes on the first screen and which section patterns are banned. An axis left unspecified fills up with the mode. Bans remain a dated linter for the remainder; positive anchoring matters more. What is bad for bans is good for a brand: moving the mode is useful if the new point belongs to that brand alone. In the brand-cloning task, references raised perceived quality and narrowed the spread ([study](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.30226)); within a brand, that is a plus. The creation skill is subject to the same thing: thin input will produce a typical warm brand, so it needs real materials, a comparison with competitors, and a person choosing among several directions. Anthropic's recommendation to propose directions before building has no numbers behind it ([vendor](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/prompt-engineering/prompting-claude-sonnet-5)), and Verbalized Sampling is proven only on text ([study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.01171v3)). The three axes must be measured separately: distance between brands, consistency within a brand, and how structure responds to the task. In Kombai, originality and range are not correlated (ρ −0.13, p=0.66).

## A single source of truth: two authored layers and one direction of generation

No candidate works as the only file. The ratings below are our inference; the cost measurements were made on design.md examples using the chars/4 estimate.

| Criterion | DESIGN.md | DTCG + Resolver | Own schema | CSS and code | Figma variables | shadcn theme |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Values | Medium: 5 groups, no shadows or modes | High: 13 types, modes, aliases | Any | High for web, almost no types | Low: 4 types, no composites | Low: string for string |
| Intent, voice, logo | Prose without structure | `$description`, `$extensions` | Best of all | Comments | Variable description | `docs`, `meta` |
| Machine checking | 11 rules with holes | Schema and Terrazzo's 27 rules | We write it ourselves | Direct check of code and render | Plugins; REST only on Enterprise | Structure only |
| Manual editing | Best | Poor: about 12 lines per color | Good in YAML | Good | GUI for designers | Medium |
| Agent reading | Cheap | About 4 times more expensive | Can be split up | Cheap | Needs MCP or an export | Cheap |
| Ecosystem | Large among agents, alpha | Large among design tools | None | Universal | Designers' standard | Large in React and Tailwind |

Independent projects already treat DESIGN.md as derived: in Impeccable, the values duplicate the CSS, and everything that does not fit lives in a sidecar ([code](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/c3bb418ee38ee49a6b999b7752c1368f3123a2bd/DESIGN.md#L5-L7)); in brandsystem-mcp, it is a portable brief ([README](https://github.com/Brandcode-Studio/brandsystem-mcp/blob/eab3927a9eee5c6bada016ccf1c0bf007af696fb/README.md#L82-L92)); in Open Design, it is prose next to compiled tokens ([code](https://github.com/nexu-io/open-design/blob/87a4f91c308170a8270458fc40fc66a86ed69aac/design-systems/_schema/manifest.schema.ts#L6-L10)).

**Recommended layout** (our inference, untested):

```text
brand/
  tokens/*.tokens.json   authored     DTCG 2025.10: values; $deprecated, $extensions for provenance
  resolver.json          authored     modes and themes
  brand.yaml             authored     voice, logo, imagery, composition, rules with IDs
  narrative/*.md         authored     rationale, North Star, examples
  decisions.log          person       who, when, why, from which evidence; append-only
  protected.yaml         person       recognizable quirks and allowed usage
  zones.yaml             person       repository zones, exemplars to copy
  evidence/*.json        tool         observed and declared; not edited by hand
build/                   generated, edits break CI
  BRAND.md, DESIGN.md, context/*.md   read by the agent
  tokens.css, Tailwind @theme, shadcn theme, import into Figma
  Stylelint, ESLint, Vale configs; check-rules.json; brand.lock (sha256 of the sources)
```

**There is one direction:** from authored files to derived ones. The reverse path from DESIGN.md to DTCG loses components, aliases, and alpha and, judging by a comparison of the code with the schema, currently produces invalid DTCG (not run through a validator). CI rebuilds `build/` and requires an empty diff. No single file should be edited by both the generator and a person. We reject the maintainer's idea that front matter wins in a conflict ([#76](https://github.com/google-labs-code/design.md/pull/76#issuecomment-4712629482)): it legitimizes hidden divergence. Until modes are merged into DESIGN.md ([PR #128](https://github.com/google-labs-code/design.md/pull/128)), the default mode goes into the front matter. We check the output with a real linter at a pinned version, as dembrandt does ([test](https://github.com/dembrandt/dembrandt/blob/90e2691af47176c600c09a956cddd2f2e82a0003/test/design-md-lint.test.ts#L8-L16)), parsing the JSON rather than the exit code. For a team without a pipeline, a single DESIGN.md is acceptable as a start, with a plan to move the values into DTCG. It is an open question whether to author tokens in YAML: Terrazzo accepts it, Figma expects JSON.

**What tokens cannot express.** No format ties a rule to a check. This is the main new part of the schema. Every rule gets an ID, an owner, a rationale, and a means of control; a field with no consumer or check is a build error, so as not to repeat `typeScale`, `clearSpace`, and `neverPair` in GoldLegend. A rule without a machine check gets the status `not_verified`, not `OK`, unlike in brandsystem-mcp ([code](https://github.com/Brandcode-Studio/brandsystem-mcp/blob/eab3927a9eee5c6bada016ccf1c0bf007af696fb/src/tools/brand-check-compliance.ts#L212-L218)).

```yaml
- id: accent-cta-only            # a sketch, not a standard
  rule: Accent only on the screen's main action
  scope: [storefront, app]
  severity: error
  check: render:accent-role      # lint:, render:, vale:, judge:, human
  owner: design-lead
  decision: D-014
```

| Layer | Form in the source | What checks it | What is left to a person |
|---|---|---|---|
| Voice | We Are / We Are Not, a tone grid, surfaces without personality, before-and-after pairs as fixtures ([code](https://github.com/GoldLegendW80/brand/blob/e2f2f4de9f2166c744a6079ce65fbf0c14e34db9/skills/make-brand/assets/editorial/03-VOICE-STORYTELLING.tmpl.md#L110-L121)); vocabulary and bans | Vocabulary deterministically ([code](https://github.com/errata-ai/Microsoft/blob/ea5fa73c530a8413f39701b01856258c0223f101/Microsoft/Wordiness.yml#L1-L9)); register by a narrow judge | Tone in contested places |
| Logo | Outline files, clear space, minimum size, allowed backgrounds, misuse | Size and clear space via the DOM, presence; SVG sanitization | Incorrect usage |
| Imagery | Positive examples, substitution tests | A judge with references, `not_verified` by default | Most of it |
| Usage rules | Records with IDs, as above | Token role by property, as in Primer ([code](https://github.com/primer/stylelint-config/blob/0dab605c559de7a49a3357af6de9b4cf8bbfa39e/plugins/colors.js#L26-L32)); heuristics like `accent-overuse` ([code](https://github.com/nexu-io/open-design/blob/87a4f91c308170a8270458fc40fc66a86ed69aac/apps/daemon/src/lint-artifact.ts#L1-L18)) | The right token in the wrong place |
| Rationale | `narrative/`, `decisions.log`, a link from the rule | Not checked; shown on conflict | Everything |
| Composition | Grid, first-screen scenarios, allowed section patterns, component registry, surface mode ([code](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/c3bb418ee38ee49a6b999b7752c1368f3123a2bd/skill/reference/mode-operate.md#L7)) | DOM pattern detectors, share of own components | Hierarchy and character |

Delivery to the agent is split up: a short, always-loaded pointer; task context from a fixed table with an explicit `no_governed_match` ([code](https://github.com/Brandcode-Studio/brandsystem-mcp/blob/eab3927a9eee5c6bada016ccf1c0bf007af696fb/src/tools/brand-context.ts#L17-L28)); details on request. `BRAND.md` holds only what the agent would not infer on its own.

## A company with a product: capture, decide, freeze, and only then change

This whole section is our proposal, assembled from sources and untested in this form. The client team's work does not stop at any stage.

**An extractor supplies evidence.** On the designlang author's benchmark, the primary color is guessed in 9 of 17 cases, by the competitor dembrandt in 12 of 17, and the body font in 25 of 27 by both ([code, author's data](https://github.com/Manavarya09/design-extract/blob/6050fdb470d87c14f3db42a07ba782ca93286c75/bench/results/2026-09-15-baseline.md#L1-L11)). Frequency is not intent: linear weights let a green CTA on 4 buttons beat GOV.UK's blue ([code](https://github.com/Manavarya09/design-extract/blob/6050fdb470d87c14f3db42a07ba782ca93286c75/src/extractors/colors.js#L145-L160)). The two leading tools weigh a declared token in opposite ways: a bonus of 120 in one ([code](https://github.com/Manavarya09/design-extract/blob/6050fdb470d87c14f3db42a07ba782ca93286c75/src/extractors/colors.js#L26-L31)), +20 in the other ([code](https://github.com/dembrandt/dembrandt/blob/90e2691af47176c600c09a956cddd2f2e82a0003/lib/extractors/colors.ts#L615-L619)). Sources diverge: a color from LinkedIn's official PDF is absent from the page ([data](https://github.com/Manavarya09/design-extract/blob/6050fdb470d87c14f3db42a07ba782ca93286c75/bench/holdout.json#L4)), and an old Stripe color from brand-assets is not rendered ([data](https://github.com/Manavarya09/design-extract/blob/6050fdb470d87c14f3db42a07ba782ca93286c75/bench/sites.json#L4)). In the UX Tools survey (n=2220, year unclear), 46.3% reported significant mismatches between specifications and components ([survey](https://www.uxtools.co/survey/design-systems/overview)). No one has measured mismatches between PDF, Figma, code, and presentations **within a single company**.

**Three layers instead of one value.** `observed`: where, how many, area, theme, surface, date, file and line; facts only. `declared`: who declared it and where (PDF, Figma, tokens, the `--brand` variable), date, author. `decided`: status, replacement, deadline, who, when, why, from which evidence; filled in only by a person. Statuses: `canon`, `tolerated` with a deadline, `deprecated` with a replacement, `forbidden`, `protected` with a rationale, `unknown`. The value `unknown` counts as neither permission nor a ban. Source precedence is not fixed in advance: a value on the site proves that it exists, a PDF that someone once wanted it.

**What is actually new here.** We did not find this separation in full, but parts of it already exist.

| Project | What already exists | What is missing |
|---|---|---|
| brandsystem-mcp | `source` and `confidence` on a value ([code](https://github.com/Brandcode-Studio/brandsystem-mcp/blob/eab3927a9eee5c6bada016ccf1c0bf007af696fb/src/schemas/core-identity.ts#L4-L18)), a catalog by source with conflicts ([code](https://github.com/Brandcode-Studio/brandsystem-mcp/blob/eab3927a9eee5c6bada016ccf1c0bf007af696fb/src/lib/source-catalog.ts#L162-L182)), `brand_clarify`, approval with sha256 | The decision is at the level of the whole brand; rank picks the winner; the code does not distinguish a person from an agent ([code](https://github.com/Brandcode-Studio/brandsystem-mcp/blob/eab3927a9eee5c6bada016ccf1c0bf007af696fb/src/tools/brand-clarify.ts#L632-L645)); provenance is lost at runtime |
| echowang97 | `observed` and `derived` with a rationale | A declared layer and a decision layer |

| cofoundy | Documents are the declared, production is the real, a distinction between debt and drift ([code](https://github.com/cofoundy/brand-skills/blob/810e69db8971a3df75acf51261bd1b49a433e943/skills/brand-audit/SKILL.md#L15-L21)) | Only an instruction to the model |

What remains new: the three layers are stored side by side and not collapsed; the decision is per value, with an owner and a deadline; the status automatically sets how strict the check is; `protected` overrides anti-slop and autofixes. Whether this helps is unknown.

**Who decides.** Each domain has one named owner; in a small company, often the founder. Logo, primary color, font, tone: the brand owner. Token semantics in the product: the design or product lead. Mapping tokens to code: the engineering lead. Marketing and presentations: the marketing lead. The agent only proposes. Without a person, you cannot choose the canon, assign `protected`, or sign off on a visible change or on a conflict between the brand and accessibility. The client logs into closed systems personally: the agent does not get passwords.

| Stage | What the agent does | What the person decides | Exit condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0. Frame | Surfaces, repositories, stacks, tokens | Owners and scope | Owners are named |
| 1. Observed | Render per "surface + theme + auth" pair, static analysis with file and line, history from git and Wayback ([code](https://github.com/cssstats/cssstats/blob/1bfbf3ae54ff8086cb068eecb5ce958a9509b1c5/packages/wayback-css/index.js#L8-L17)), a degradation log | Spot checks: is this the right product | What was not measured is marked as missing |
| 2. Declared | PDF, Figma, tokens, presentations brought into one form | Hands over materials, marks what is outdated | Sources are dated |
| 3. Conflict register | Matches them up; flags only mechanics on its own: twins with ΔE <1 ([code](https://github.com/dembrandt/dembrandt/blob/90e2691af47176c600c09a956cddd2f2e82a0003/lib/findings.ts#L49-L72)) | Nothing yet | Every conflict has become a question |
| 4. Interview and decisions | 20–30 minutes, shows the evidence, writes the log | The status of every value | Key roles are decided |
| 5. Encoding | `primitive`, `semantic`, `legacy alias`; an "old → new" table with a confidence level; `zones.yaml` | Contested mappings, exemplars | Confirmed on examples |
| 6. Freeze | `error` with a baseline, legacy markers, a check of added lines, a hook, a VR baseline | What blocks now | Old code does not fail, a new violation fails |
| 7. Replacement without changing the visuals | Small PRs only for deterministic mappings | Merging | Zero VR diff |
| 8. Intentional changes | Separate PRs linking to a decision | Every visible change | Changes are tied to decisions |
| 9. Ratchet | Removes suppressions, raises strictness, checks against the live site | `tolerated` deadlines | Coverage grows, exceptions do not go stale |

For a small team, one marketing page and one screen are enough; stages 3–4 fit into one session, and 7–9 happen along the way whenever code is touched, as at Harvey ([self-report](https://www.harvey.ai/blog/rebuilding-harveys-design-system-from-the-ground-up)). The freeze and the decision log are required in any variant.

**Protecting quirks.** Distinctiveness lives in buyers' memory; it cannot be inferred from CSS ([Ehrenberg-Bass](https://marketingscience.info/news-and-insights/brands-need-distinctive-assets)). Recognizable ugliness and accidental debt look the same in the data. An interface example: Bloomberg guards black with amber as part of its brand ([self-report, 2017](https://digitalcontentnext.org/?p=14856)); we found no causal data on the benefit of such quirks in digital products. Individual signals of slop detectors fire on real brands: `indigo-gradient` on Shopify, `hero-three-cards` on gov.uk ([author's data](https://github.com/Manavarya09/design-extract/blob/6050fdb470d87c14f3db42a07ba782ca93286c75/bench/results/slop-2026-11-04.md#L15-L27)). We tell them apart by a combination of signs, and this is an untested heuristic: it has lasted through years and redesigns; it lives on several surfaces; it is applied consistently, as one value; someone can explain and defend it; it is rare in the category. No single fact decides, because inertia also lasts for years. The result is a candidate with evidence and a question for a person. The record: `protected: {asset, why, evidence, owner, date, allowed_uses, review_date}`. All anti-slop rules and autofixes must read it; until the brand is described, they work only as warnings. Poor contrast in a brand color is not a verdict but a constraint: the color stays on color blocks and leaves the text; a person decides. A team should not judge its own assets alone: marketers overestimate fame and underestimate uniqueness ([study, abstract](https://doi.org/10.1057/s41262-025-00395-y)).

**Agent behavior in legacy code.** Neither "follow the neighboring code" nor "always write to the new brand" works. We found no direct controlled data on agents copying design patterns. There is indirect data: Codex repeats the repository's patterns, including suboptimal ones (a secondhand retelling of an OpenAI post; the original did not open; [review](https://yage.ai/share/harness-engineering-survey-en-20260312.html)).

| Zone | What to do | What is forbidden |
|---|---|---|
| New file or component | Canon only; exemplars only from `exemplars` | Taking neighboring legacy as the exemplar |
| Edit in legacy | Local structure, minimal diff, new lines without new off-canon values | Redesigning along the way, copying raw values |
| Value with a deterministic mapping | Replace the touched lines if the visuals do not change | Replacing ambiguous mappings without confirmation |
| No token or unclear role | A `needs-decision` record with evidence | Silently taking the nearest token |
| `protected` | Follow `allowed_uses` | "Improving" it |
| Generated code, widgets, CMS | Do not touch; record the source | Autofixes |

Conflicts are resolved from the top down: law and accessibility, with escalation to a person; `protected`; the decision log; canon; local code; best practice.

**Migration without breakage.** The two phases do not mix. Phase A preserves the visuals: an alias or `var(token, old value)` ([code](https://github.com/salesforce-ux/slds-linter/blob/7904c5e0c18d567681d7e511e4768af480dbd8f8/packages/eslint-plugin-react-slds/src/utils/no-hardcoded-values/handlers/color-handler.ts#L45-L49)); a nonzero visual diff counts as an error. Phase B changes values intentionally, and every change is tied to a decision. Autofix is split into levels, as in SLDS: deterministic, semi-deterministic, llm, no-hooks ([README](https://github.com/salesforce-ux/slds-linter/blob/7904c5e0c18d567681d7e511e4768af480dbd8f8/docs/no-hardcoded-values-rule-options.md#L425-L434)); we apply only the first automatically. We roll back partial edits, as Polaris does ([code](https://github.com/Shopify/polaris/blob/3f7954ae42fabf26d63cee68c23ceebfd7ef0972/polaris-migrator/src/utilities/sass.ts#L507-L515)). We do not guess colors: at Atlassian, the suggestion inserts an empty `token('')` ([code, npm 16.16.0](https://registry.npmjs.org/@atlaskit/eslint-plugin-design-system/-/eslint-plugin-design-system-16.16.0.tgz)). The cost of guessing shows at Zalando: the old button's "medium" visually equaled the new one's "large" ([self-report](https://engineering.zalando.com/posts/2025/02/llm-migration-ui-component-libraries.html)).

The ratchet is built from three parts: `error` plus ESLint or Stylelint suppressions ([code](https://github.com/eslint/eslint/blob/17f17cd7a248c532cfce61b7f915760608ca7121/lib/services/suppressions-service.js#L128-L176), [experimental](https://github.com/stylelint/stylelint/blob/994bc5604223d3315d7cfe80e2240418ad80434c/docs/user-guide/suppressions.md#L3-L4)); a legacy marker in the code that the agent sees when reading (`DO NOT COPY`, [code](https://github.com/Shopify/polaris/blob/3f7954ae42fabf26d63cee68c23ceebfd7ef0972/polaris-migrator/src/migrations/styles-insert-stylelint-disable/transform.ts#L10-L34)); a check of added lines, because a per-file counter will not notice one violation being swapped for another ([inference]). Suppressions do not count `warn` ([code](https://github.com/eslint/eslint/blob/17f17cd7a248c532cfce61b7f915760608ca7121/docs/src/use/suppressions.md#L14-L16)), so new violations must be `error`. We found no measured cases on the cost of migration or the growth of coverage. Coinbase's numbers come from a clean codebase ([vendor](https://www.figma.com/blog/how-coinbase-used-code-connect-to-shrink-token-costs/)) and prove nothing for brownfield.

## How to measure: compliance and distinctiveness are different questions, and six brands confirm nothing

### Which instruction layers agents obey

There are no direct brand measurements for any layer. All the data come from other targets: API knowledge, bug fixing, format constraints, trivial annotations. Transfer to a brand book is not proven.

| Layer | Best data | Caveat |
|---|---|---|
| Always-loaded file | Vercel: baseline 53%, skill 53%, skill with an instruction 79%, an index in AGENTS.md 100% ([vendor](https://vercel.com/blog/agents-md-outperforms-skills-in-our-agent-evals)). Gloaguen: instructions are followed, but success does not rise significantly; cost +20% and +23% for LLM-written files, up to +19% for developer-written files ([study](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.11988)) | Vercel does not state the number of tasks and repeats; the target is fresh APIs. Gloaguen: only Python, no style |
| On-demand skill | SkillsBench: 33.9% → 50.5%, invocation from 46.4% to 99.2% ([study](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.12670)). SWE-Skills-Bench: 76.59% → 76.93%, 92 of 98 public skills ineffective ([study](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.15401)) | The first uses curated skills from the top quartile, the second public ones as they are. Agent-written skills fall below baseline: −8.1, −11.3, −11.5 pp |
| MCP | Storybook +12.8% ([vendor](https://storybook.js.org/blog/storybook-mcp-for-react/)); Bill: −45% tokens, n=1 per cell ([vendor](https://www.bill.com/engineering/how-we-made-our-design-system-bilingual)) | No independent comparisons. Schemas of about 10000 tokens explained 62–100% of the weakening of constraints on two models ([single-author preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.20911)) |
| Hooks | Deterministic control, per the documentation ([vendor](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/hooks-guide)); activation 100% with forced-eval versus 50–55% without the hook ([blog, small n](https://scottspence.com/posts/measuring-claude-code-skill-activation-with-sandboxed-evals)) | No controlled comparison of a hook versus an instruction on UI. `PostToolUse` does not block, exit 1 does not block ([docs](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/hooks)) |
| CI and an external verifier | Linter reports over 10 iterations cut the share of problems from >40% to 13% ([study, abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.14419)) | The domain is not UI. The agent optimizes for the check: GPT-5 passes impossible tests in 76% and 54%, Opus 4.1 in 50% ([study](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.20270)) |

Compliance drops within a session. In a factorial study on a trivial annotation, each additional function lowers the odds of compliance by about 5.6% (OR=0.944), while file size and instruction position showed no notable contrasts ([study, abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10039)); the Claude Code documentation, meanwhile, advises up to 200 lines, without numbers ([vendor](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/memory)). Bans erode with dialogue depth, while requirements hold: for Mistral Large 3, a ban is at 73% on turn 5 and 33% on turn 16 ([preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.20911)). Compactors keep on average 17% of the constraints set in the session ([study, abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11242)). Hence a practical hypothesis: "use `var(--x)`" holds longer than "never use hex," and critical rules must be stored outside the compressible context.

Our reliability hierarchy, from strongest to weakest: an external verifier and CI that the agent cannot edit; a hook with blocking and feedback; a CLI or MCP as the only convenient path to the values; a short always-loaded pointer; an on-demand skill. This is an order of reliability, not of value: the upper layers catch values, the lower ones carry intent. You can enforce hex, font, grid step, component. You cannot enforce hierarchy, character, or tone.

### Metrics and their traps

Compliance answers the question "do the values match the brand." Distinctiveness answers the question "can this be recognized among others." Quality is a third question. A registry is confirmed for the first, references are likely for the second, a visual loop for the third ([inference]). We do not fold them into one score: a single number hides the trade of compliance for character and immediately becomes a target for the agent.

| Family | What to measure | Main traps |
|---|---|---|
| Deterministic | Off-token by area, hard-coded values in the diff, token coverage, font by rendered glyphs via `CSS.getPlatformFontsForNode` ([code](https://github.com/ChromeDevTools/devtools-protocol/blob/b0d96c6cdf39e5583e67a3762b2815a33154455b/json/browser_protocol.json)) | A correct value in the wrong role passes. 0% off-token can be reached by giving up color. SVG and canvas are invisible in computed style. No one has shown a link to on-brand judgment |
| Perceptual | ΔE00, DINOv2 embeddings with text and logo masked | The threshold depends on size ([code](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/d3-jnd@0.1.3/README.md)); plain CLIP encodes content, UIClip at picking the best is 75.12% ([study](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2404.12500)). A brand is not defined by one page; similarity to a reference does not transfer |
| Distinctiveness | Brand attribution from masked screenshots, silhouette within a task, relation to the default centroid | Without masking, a classifier guesses by the logo: 95.82% versus 66.03% on the whole screenshot ([study](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.19598v2)). There are no norms; a threshold only as a difference from placebo |
| People | Blind matching after showing a board; pairwise "which fits the brand better" | Designers' agreement on preferences is low: alpha 0.25 ([study](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.20513v1)) and 0.37. They agree more on faithfulness to a description than on aesthetics ([study](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.20731)). Ask "does it fit," not "do you like it" |
| LLM judge | Narrow checkable questions for triage | Six open VLMs and three specialized scorers agree with the majority of 5 designers in 0.499–0.543 of cases, with chance at 0.5 ([study](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.20731)); there are no frontier models in this comparison; on "requirement met," kappa is 0.86 and 0.84 ([study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.06251)). Position bias ([study](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.07791v9)), manipulation +80.2% ([study](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2505.15249)). | Buyer memory | Fame and Uniqueness for choosing assets ([Ehrenberg-Bass](https://marketingscience.info/brands-of-distinction)) | A new small brand will score about zero on any output: useless as an agent test |

No open UI benchmark measures brand faithfulness. The closest model is VIST: blind, which of two images better matches 10 brand references, but these are illustrations, and the source is a labeling vendor's blog ([vendor](https://toloka.ai/blog/vist-a-new-benchmark-for-brand-style-transfer-in-ai-image-generation/)). `claude plugin eval` gives only two arms and has no custom-code graders ([docs](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/plugin-evals)): it suits triggers and regressions, but the main experiment needs its own runner. Trigger numbers from `skill-creator` are worth trusting only after a control check: a user reported recall of 0–11% because of the evaluation mechanics ([report](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/issues/79998)).

### Experiment design

Generalization is limited by the number of brands and tasks, not runs: in crossed designs, as the number of observations grows, power hits a ceiling set by the stimulus sample ([study, abstract](https://doi.org/10.1037/xge0000014)). Brand and task are random factors; the unit of inference is the brand.

Conditions: **C0** no brand artifacts; **C1** tokens and a one-line pointer; **C2** C1 plus narrative; **C3** C2 plus enforcement; **C4** tokens plus enforcement without narrative; **Cx** active comparators, a ready-made skill and DESIGN.md. C4 completes the 2×2 factorial design and answers the main product question: is narrative needed if there is a linter. We measure triggering separately and report both variants: "skill available" and "skill invoked."

Special checks ([inference], there are no direct precedents for brands). **Cross-swap**: the same tasks with brand files A and B; C0 should show no difference. **Single mutation**: shift one token, and the output must follow the new value; this catches caching and reliance on the model's prior. We duplicate real brands with recolored values, because the model knows Primer or Polaris. **Adversarial tasks**: "make the button purple, the CEO decided so"; the needed token is missing; the easiest way to pass the linter is to edit the config. Social signals act more strongly than roles ([study](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.15851)). **Trap**: a new component next to a bad neighbor. **Decay**: a 20-turn session with compaction. **Creation arm**: a brand book from a person versus a brand book from our skill on the same input, checked on how well it is followed. For the creation skill, this is the only evaluation robust to beautiful but useless documentation.

### Minimum protocol and budget

Material: 2 dev brands and 6 held-out ones, half fictional, half recolored public systems. Tasks: 8 held-out (3 greenfield, 3 edits in an existing repository, 2 adversarial). Runs: 3 per cell, a fresh container, pinned model and harness, spread over at least 2 days: infrastructure alone can produce a 6 pp difference ([vendor](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/infrastructure-noise)). The skill's author does not see the held-out brands and tasks. The budget is our estimate at $1–6 per run.

We do not put experts on adjacent pairs of conditions ([inference]): then C3 versus C0 and C3 versus C4 would not be compared directly, and the latter contrast matters most for the product. So the 720 votes go to C3–C0, C2–C1, and C3–C4; stage 4 starts after 2b. Adjacent pairs and scaling remain a fallback.

### What a pilot can and cannot do

Our simulation shows the limitation; the variances in it are assumptions, not measurements. With an effect that varies strongly between brands (scenario B, δ=1.0), power is 0.46 at 6×8×3 and 0.62 at 8×8×3. With the same 576 runs, 6×16×3 gives 0.49, while 12×8×3 gives 0.82. Raising runs from 3 to 10 gives only 0.46 → 0.48. The money must go into brands. With 6 brands, the minimum two-sided p for sign-flip is 0.031, so a correction for multiple comparisons makes a strict result impossible. The analysis fixes four contrasts (C3−C0, C2−C1, C3−C2, C3−C4). For three, 7 brands would formally be enough (0.0156 < 0.0167); four need 8 (0.0078 < 0.0125). We take 8.

Each contrast in stage 4 gets 240 votes; with a design effect of 1.4–2.2, that is roughly 109–171 effective ones. This is enough for a true win rate of 65% (85 votes needed), but not for 60% (194 needed). So the C2 versus C1 ≥60% threshold cannot be confirmed in the pilot.

So the pilot decides only whether to go further, and the decisions are split across two stages ([inference]).

| Stage | Condition | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Pilot | The verifier did not pass stage 0 | Fix it; compliance numbers cannot be trusted |
| Pilot | G1–G4 or G11–G13 not reached | Enforcement does not steer the output: review the transcripts, rework |
| Pilot | G7 failed or G21 exceeded | Enforcement breaks the work or rewards workarounds: rework before confirmation |
| Pilot | G10 or the human measures go negative | An alarm signal, not a stop: the intervals are too wide. Review, and recompute power on the real variances |
| Pilot | G1–G4, G7–G9, G11–G13 within norms, the direction of G14 or G17 is right | Go to confirmation. This is not proof |
| Confirmation | The same plus G14 or G17, contrasts with the Holm correction | Go |
| Confirmation | Compliance passed, no distinctiveness, or C4≈C3 with people | Linter and tokens only: remove or rework the narrative |
| Confirmation | C3≫C4 on values, but C2≈C1 with people | Rework the narrative, run the new version on the dev brands |
| Confirmation | G7 or G10 failed at any compliance thresholds | Stop |
| Confirmation | Superiority over Cx on held-out brands on G14 or G18, and no worse on G1–G4 | Only now can one claim "better than existing solutions" |

With a different number of brands, the chance level in G14 changes, and the threshold must be recomputed. The primary test is sign-flip or a paired t on brand means, intervals come from a cluster bootstrap, and a mixed model serves as support. We show the distribution across brands and tasks and review negative cells through the transcripts. The verifier lives outside the agent's repository, the token file is read-only, and a legitimate answer "no suitable token, escalating" is required: a flag option cut workarounds by GPT-5 from 54% to 9% ([study](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.20270)). Some metrics are not shown to the agent. There is a tension here: `brand check` is part of the product, while the reference verifier in the eval must be broader than it and hidden.

## Architecture: a file-based compiler with one check and thin adapters

Everything in this section is our inference, untested in this form. The build-cost estimates are relative: there are no measurements.

| Criterion | A. Skill + DESIGN.md | B. File-based compiler, skills, lint, hooks | C. MCP server as runtime | D. Hosted platform | E. Rule packs for Impeccable |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Enforcement | Low: everything rests on the model | High for values; meaning only by a judge and a person | Medium: the agent is free not to call it if there is no hook | High within its own generation, zero outside | Medium: the hook advises, CI exit 2 is wired up by hand |
| Brownfield | Weak: a description without a freeze | Strong, if there is adopt and a ratchet | Medium: extraction exists, a freeze does not | Medium: good code import, but inside the product | Medium: inherits the world, no decision layer |
| Portability | High for reading | High: CLI and CI everywhere, hook semantics differ | Only clients with MCP; instructions get truncated | Low | 21 configurations, but the detector is web-only |
| Context | From low to 80 KB | Low: a pointer plus check output | 3.35–10.1k tokens for descriptions, estimate | Outside our context | Engine almost zero; skill 6–35k, estimate |
| Build cost | Low | Medium or high | High | Very high | Low or medium |
| Lock-in | To an alpha format, weak | Low: files and standard linters | Medium | High | Medium: `publish = false`, a git dependency |
| Main risk | Zero effect: public skills get +0.34 | False positives and workarounds; character is not caught | The agent does not call it; heavy schemas weaken bans | Competition with Frontify, Adobe, Lovable | Someone else's API; anti-slop argues with the brand |

**Recommendation: B.** Only it combines a check that does not depend on the model's discipline, a low context cost, and work in an existing repository. A becomes the Cx comparator and a free by-product: we generate `DESIGN.md` anyway. C is added later as a thin adapter to the same command: in Atlassian's internal benchmark, CLI versus MCP gave 352 → 325 s and 218k → 201k tokens per task ([vendor](https://www.atlassian.com/blog/ai-at-work/giving-ai-agents-design-system-context-from-the-terminal-what-we-learned-building-a-cli)), that is, about 8%, and the main argument for the CLI is reaching agents without an MCP client ([inference]). E we take as an export target, not as a dependency. D is not for a small team: a read-only MCP to a portal has become a commodity, but it can be consumed as an input.

```text
code and render ────▶ observed ─┐
PDF, Figma, tokens ─▶ declared ─┴─▶ conflict register ─▶ a human decision ─▶ decisions.log
                                                                                  │
interview, materials ─▶ tokens/ + brand.yaml + narrative/ ◀───────────────────────┘
                                   │
                              brand build
        ┌──────────────────────────┼─────────────────────────────┐
        ▼                          ▼                             ▼
BRAND.md, DESIGN.md,      tokens.css, Tailwind,       linter configs,
context/ — intent         shadcn — correct values     check-rules
                                                                 │
                                                       brand check — one verdict
                          ┌───────────────┬──────────────────────┤
                          ▼               ▼                      ▼
                    PostToolUse:     Stop: exit 2,       CI: required check
                    errors to agent  cannot finish       outside agent's reach
```

**Components.** Schema and compiler: JSON Schema with a contract version, Terrazzo for tokens, a field with no consumer is an error. `brand check`: lint of added lines, render (computed styles, rendered fonts, pixels, a dev-server URL), voice vocabulary, an optional narrow judge; statuses `pass`, `fail`, `not_verified`, `inconclusive`; a check with zero rules does not pass. A finding contains a `fix` for the agent ([code](https://github.com/nexu-io/open-design/blob/87a4f91c308170a8270458fc40fc66a86ed69aac/packages/contracts/src/api/artifact-lint.ts#L10-L52)) and an instruction on how to choose. Adapters: in Claude Code, `PreToolUse` forbids editing tokens and suppressions, `PostToolUse` returns errors, and Stop with code 2 prevents finishing ([docs](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/hooks)); in Cursor, `preToolUse` and `postToolUse` with `additional_context` ([docs](https://cursor.com/docs/agent/hooks)). We did not check the hooks of Codex, Copilot, and Windsurf. Skills: one visible router and hidden `brand-create`, `brand-adopt`, `brand-review`, as in cofoundy ([code](https://github.com/cofoundy/brand-skills/blob/810e69db8971a3df75acf51261bd1b49a433e943/scripts/validate-skills.sh#L18-L30)). Critical content goes at the start of files: after compaction, the first 5000 tokens of a skill are reattached ([docs](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/skills)). Eval loop: our own runner and a closed verifier.

### Build order

| Step | What we build | Why exactly now | Exit |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Verifier, 2 dev brands, measurement of C0 and Cx | Without it, no result can be checked | Protocol stage 0 passed |
| 1 | Schema, compiler, tokens → CSS, Tailwind, shadcn, `DESIGN.md`, `BRAND.md` | Gives C1 and C2 | The output passes the linters at pinned versions |
| 2 | `brand check`, Claude Code Stop hook, CI, Russian font check | The product's main bet | C3 and C4 ready for the pilot |
| 3 | Pilot on 6 brands | Decision on whether to continue | Decision table |
| 4 | `brand-adopt`: observed, declared, decided, ratchet | The largest unoccupied area | 2–3 real companies |
| 5 | Voice: Russian vocabulary, then a narrow judge | Tone cannot be enforced, so we start with the vocabulary | Before-and-after fixtures |
| 6 | `brand-create` | Generated instructions can be worse than none | The "person versus skill" arm |
| 7 | Confirmation on 8–12 brands; MCP and Cursor adapters, rule pack export | The right to claim "better" | A public benchmark |

A public, reproducible brand-faithfulness benchmark will by itself set the product apart: none of the projects reviewed has one.

**What would change the recommendation.** C4≈C3 with people: the product comes down to a linter and tokens. C3≫C4: the main investment goes into a composition grammar and narrative. Clients work in Lovable or v0, where our hooks do not exist: registry and MCP adapters are needed, and C's share grows. The buyers are marketers, and the surfaces are emails, slides, and PDFs: the file-based loop does not reach them, and the arguments for D get stronger. Impeccable turns on brand rules by default and stabilizes its package API: E becomes cheaper. DESIGN.md gains `imports`, modes, and a configurable linter: its role grows, although the value layer stays in DTCG.

**Stop criteria (ours).** After two iterations, the verifier does not reach recall ≥95% with false positives ≤2%: enforcement is not ready. G1 or G11–G13 are not reached after a rework on the dev brands: the brand file does not steer the output. G7 or G10 fail at confirmation: enforcement breaks the work or the taste. At confirmation, there is no superiority over Cx: we drop the claim "better" and either sell it as a linter or shut it down. In the field test, teams turn off the hook because of false positives: we revise strictness and rules before any growth. At two of three real companies, the decision session does not produce a canon: we simplify `brand-adopt`.

## Limits of the research: what was not run and what the claims rest on

**Nothing was run.** All findings in code come from reading; regex probes were run outside the clones, on our own strings. These depend on runtime: the invisible logos and PPTX in GoldLegend, the exit code of `design.md lint` on broken YAML, the advisory filter in the Impeccable hook, the displacement of `--brand-primary` in brandsystem-mcp. We did not measure the accuracy of extractors, detectors, or judges. The chars/4 and 4-bytes-per-token figures are estimates. We did not measure `tools/list` live.

**The Krebs recount is ours, and the data disagree.** The [post](https://adriankrebs.ch/blog/design-slop/) has 1590 pages and a 22/32/46 split (4+, 2–3, 0–1 patterns), the repository files have 1390 pages and 20.4/32.5/47.1, and the correlation uses 1369; the detector's README speaks of 14 patterns, while we counted 15 labels in the files. The split into surface and structure is debatable. Links to the Krebs and dani-z repositories point to the current version, without a SHA. The recount script (`krebs_ban.py`) and the power simulation script (`power_sim.py`) sit with the working notes in `docs/research/notes/agent-brand-systems/`.

**Could not open:** the Stitch page (title only), the zeroheight and catchdrift help pages (403), the OpenAI post (403; a secondary review was used instead), the Goree PDF and the full texts of Cha et al. and Hintze, the getdrio and socket.dev pages; `sunil-dsb/design.md`, `sylwaninn/ds-drift`, `thefilatovigor/filatov-brandbook-skill` did not clone. We did not read the reasons for the Socket and Snyk warnings on skills.sh. We consider the mechanics of zeroheight, DriftSense, and catchdrift unconfirmed and do not use them. We did not read Paper's primary documents and did not check the status of Claude Design as of November.

**Abstract only:** arXiv 2605.10039, 2608.11242, 2508.14419, 2611.01549, Brus et al., Westfall et al. **Secondary retelling only:** the harness engineering review; it was used as indirect evidence. Materials known only from snippets (OverlayQA, Atomize, Snyk on Copilot) did not enter the conclusions. **Vendor self-reports:** Vercel, Storybook, Bill, Atlassian, Coinbase/Figma, Zalando, Harvey, Lovable, GenStudio, Kombai (sells a UI product), VIST. The Lovable and GenStudio numbers cannot be checked from outside. The 2026 preprints have not been peer-reviewed; 2604.20911 was written by a single author.

**Disagreements within the sources themselves.** Cursor: the Impeccable site says writes can be blocked ([site](https://impeccable.style/docs/hooks)), while its code and `hooks.md` say the opposite ([code](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/c3bb418ee38ee49a6b999b7752c1368f3123a2bd/crates/hook/src/before_edit.rs#L1-L8)). Color tolerances are not aligned at all: ΔE 2.3 in render checks, ΔE 2 in our G1, ±6 per channel in Impeccable, RGB 50 in ui-ux-pro-max, 15 in extract-design-system; these are starting values, not norms.

**Deliberately not used.** Popular figures on the share of blue-purple colors and on the share of Show HN pages with signs of AI, figures from practice on the effect of hooks taken from search results, the claim that Show HN tripled: the method is not described, or the source is outdated. We take the indigo-500 story as the author's joke, not as data. We do not cite Ward et al. and Luther et al.: they are confirmed only by snippets. We consider figures from extracted system prompts likely but unconfirmed.
