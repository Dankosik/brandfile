# Visual identity craft: where the norm rules, where the eye judges, where the lawyer decides

Updated: November 6, 2026.
Status: a synthesis of desk research and local tests for training AI agents. Some sources were read only through a summary or search results; the text marks this. On November 6, 2026, the key legal citations, amounts, thresholds, prices, and standard statuses went through a second cross-check against primary sources; what remains unverified is listed in the "Could not verify" section. Legal sections: a method based on research, not legal advice; no patent attorney has reviewed them. Thresholds marked "our threshold" are not validated.

## Conclusion

**How professionals choose.** We found no single verified canon for color, type, or the mark. Color is picked by eye or computed from contrast; we treat these schools as stages of one job: a human sets the axis and the character, the machine builds the scale and checks the thresholds, a human accepts the result. Type is chosen by working from the task toward a minimum of families with fixed roles, while the direction of a pairing remains taste. For the mark there is Wheeler's stage scheme (one author, read in a Logo Design Love summary), and it has no separate test stage; how Landor, Pentagram, Koto, and Wolff Olins work could not be confirmed. The real norm is narrow: the WCAG contrast formula, the OpenType, WOFF2, and SVG specifications, the texts of licenses and the law; CSS Fonts and CSS Color are still drafts (section 1).

**How rules are written.** A strong guideline stands out by a rule with a number, a unit the tolerance is tied to, and an exception route. The partner documents of Spotify, Shopify, and Sber are more precise than corporate pages, because they address people the brand does not control; an agent is in the same position. Clear space, the logo, and tone are not described as a type in any of the four token formats reviewed, so a separate rules file is needed (section 2).

**What the machine checks and what it does not.** Presence and numeric conformance are checked deterministically: the contrast of two known colors, ΔE, cmap, metadata, SVG validity, tokens, pixel changes. The main risk is not a wrong number but a missing verdict and swapping the question "does it conform" for "is it good". The quality of Cyrillic letterforms, kerning, fit with positioning, and the choice of the final mark stay with a human. Similarity of marks, relatedness of goods and services, and font rights stay with a lawyer. We found no share of automation for visual identity (section 3).

**Cyrillic.** 28 of the 29 families from the initial list are in google/fonts under OFL 1.1; Golos UI is not there. OFL settles the question of payment and of permission for a logo, but not the risks of distributing the file (RFN, subsetting, apps), and it does not guarantee quality; no OFL case law was found in Russian courts. The type.today experts, who have a commercial interest, advise against the Cyrillic of Inter, Manrope, Jost, Source Sans, Open Sans, and, per a 2021 review, Noto Serif. Logo use cannot be described by one rule: at type.today and Swiss Typefaces (with conditions) it is part of the base license, while Contrast, TypeType, Brownfox, and Paratype (for fonts under its own license) require a separate one. Courts consistently recognize a typeface as a work of graphic art, and since 04.01.2026 compensation in a fixed amount under Art. 1301 of the Civil Code is from 10 thousand to 10 million rubles per infringement. This is not an overall ceiling: for removing technical protection measures or deleting copyright management information it is up to 11 million rubles (para. 2), for several works on one medium up to 20 million rubles (para. 4 of Art. 1252.1), and the double-value calculation, in a practitioner's opinion, has no limit (section 4).

**Trademarks.** A preliminary check produces a risk record with the database, the date, and the gaps, not a conclusion that the name is "free". The working combination: Rospatent's search platform, then the open FIPS registers; applications become visible with a delay, and WIPO's terms forbid automated queries. The minimum fees for a mark in one class with a list of up to 10 items come to 35,000 rubles per the Rospatent table as of 06.11.2026. Art. 10.1 of the Consumer Protection Law has been in force since March 1, 2026. Its para. 4 excludes company names, trademarks, and service marks. The word «зарегистрированные» ("registered") is not in the text, but the Rospotrebnadzor Guidance of 02.03.2026 and most commentators speak of registered marks, so registration is what to rely on: unregistered Latin script in a consumer setting stays in the risk zone, and the conclusion is for a lawyer (section 5).

**What not to repeat.** 60-30-10 as a law, "90% of judgments depend on color", the golden ratio of logos as a mark of quality, "a typeface speeds up reading by 35%", Gap's $100 million loss, the logo's harm in the Tropicana case (section 6).

**What this means for agents.** Agents should be taught not to "judge beauty" but to split a question into a measurable part and a remainder, and to report what checked each part. A rule is written with a condition of application, a threshold, a check method, and a third outcome, "cannot decide", which never counts as success (section 7).

## How to read the report

This report is about craft and verifiability. What a brand book contains, its medium, and the reasons it goes unused are covered in the [companion document "Anatomy of a good brand book"](brand-book-anatomy.md) and are not repeated here.

### Palette: the system computes the scale, the fit of a color remains a judgment

**The process diverges along four axes.** `[inference]` Eye versus algorithm; the entry point (brand heritage, contrast, role in the interface); preserving the exact brand color; what a step number means. `[opinion; secondary]` Albers teaches through exercises and direct perception, not through color systems ([Yale University Press](https://yalebooks.yale.edu/book/9780300179354/interaction-of-color/)). `[vendor]` Radix assigns a use case to each step, Leonardo derives colors from a target contrast; `[consensus]` the agreement is weak, there are only two systems ([Radix](https://www.radix-ui.com/colors/docs/palette-composition/understanding-the-scale), [Leonardo](https://github.com/adobe/leonardo/blob/main/packages/contrast-colors/README.md)).

**How the scales are built is reliable as a fact about the product, but it is not a norm.** Table caption: `[vendor]`; calculations are marked `[test; calculation]`.

| System | Structure | How the scale is derived | What is promised about contrast and where it breaks |
|---|---|---|---|

`[inference]` For a brand, what matters most is whether its color is preserved. Material's TONAL_SPOT fixes the chroma, so the brand color usually does not come out as primary; it is preserved more accurately in the primaryContainer of the FIDELITY and CONTENT variants and in Radix step 9, while in Leonardo the key color may not make it into the output. An agent needs a color-preserving mode. `[test; calculation]` OKLCH is more even than HSL but does not guarantee contrast: at L = 0.6 and C = 0.15, contrast on white ranges from 3.66:1 to 4.28:1 without gamut clipping and from 3.43:1 to 4.29:1 after clipping to sRGB, while HSL with L = 50% ranges from 1.07:1 to 8.59:1 ([Ottosson](https://bottosson.github.io/posts/oklab/)).

**Contrast: the WCAG 2.x formula is normative.** `[standard]` Contrast equals (L1 + 0.05) / (L2 + 0.05), ranging from 1:1 to 21:1 ([WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/)). `[standard; informative]` No rounding: 4.499:1 does not pass 4.5:1; replacing the 0.03928 threshold with 0.04045 has no practical effect ([Understanding 1.4.3](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html)). Large text starts at 18 pt, or 14 pt bold, that is, at 24 px and about 18.5 px. Table caption: `[standard; informative]`, Understanding pages; the thresholds themselves are normative.

| Criterion | Level | Threshold | Exceptions and boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| [1.4.1 Use of Color](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/use-of-color.html) | A | Color is not the only means of conveying meaning | If the color itself must be understood (green means "correct"), a second cue is needed regardless of contrast |
| [1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum)](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html) | AA | 4.5:1; large text 3:1 | Inactive elements, decoration, invisible text, text in a picture with significant other visual content, logos |
| [1.4.6 Contrast (Enhanced)](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-enhanced.html) | AAA | 7:1; large text 4.5:1 | The same |
| [1.4.11 Non-text Contrast](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/non-text-contrast.html) | AA | 3:1 against adjacent colors for components, states, meaningful graphics | Inactive elements, appearance set by the browser, graphics whose particular presentation is essential. There is no normative rule on gradients |

**There is no blanket exception for logos.** `[standard]` The norm in 1.4.3 and 1.4.6 exempts only text: "Text that is part of a logo or brand name has no contrast requirement" ([W3C](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html)). `[standard; informative]` The understanding of the boundary comes from Understanding: the exception does not extend to other text just because a brand book says so, and it does not cover low contrast the author chose; for a logo that works as a control, a high-contrast variant is recommended. In 1.4.11 the word "logo" does not appear in the norm; the exemption comes from Understanding, and a focus indicator around a logo must pass the threshold ([1.4.11](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/non-text-contrast.html)). `[inference]` When creating a new mark, the exception is useless as a justification; a reasonable internal target is 3:1 and 4.5:1 for the base versions (our threshold).

Lc figures must not be passed off as a standard. `[standard]` For Russian government websites, the same numbers are fixed by Resolution No. 102; it does not extend to a commercial brand outside that circle. `[standard; draft]` CSS Color 4 and 5 are not yet Recommendations ([Color 4](https://www.w3.org/TR/css-color-4/), [Color 5](https://www.w3.org/TR/css-color-5/)).

**Print and fabric: there is no single brand color across all media.** This is where we could verify the least: the ISO standards themselves are paid. `[research; snippet]` In ISO 12647-2:2013 the tolerance for C, M, Y, K solids is 3.5; 3.5; 3.5; 5 ΔE00 ([TAGA 2015](https://repository.rit.edu/other/854)), while an RIT dissertation argues that it is only informative and that its derivation is not justified ([Yu 2015](https://repository.rit.edu/theses/8640)). `[vendor; snippet]` In 2022 Adobe announced that most Pantone libraries would disappear from Creative Cloud and that a Pantone Connect license would be needed ([Keypoint Intelligence](https://keypointintelligence.com/keypoint-blogs/adobe-to-remove-pantone-color-libraries)). `[standard]` ISO 105-J03:2009 computes ΔE CMC(l:c); the buyer chooses the tolerance ([SCC, catalog record](https://ccn-scc.ca/standardsdb/standards/8146081)). `[vendor]` Trade sources warn that TPG and TCX may not match, so TCX and a lab dip are what gets approved ([Maker's Row](https://makersrow.com/?p=20782), [Fumao](https://fumaofabric.com/?p=19037)). `[inference]` A fashion line needs three references: screen (sRGB and P3), paper and packaging (Pantone or CMYK with a profile), fabric (TCX and a lab dip). A bare "ΔE ≤ 2" cannot be checked: it needs a formula, conditions, and a material. The brand book stores the Pantone number and the values for every medium separately, without relying on the library inside the file.

**Color vision: the figure is confirmed, there is no distinguishability threshold.** `[research]` About 8% of men and 0.4% of women of European descent ([Birch 2012](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/search?query=ext_id:22472762&resultType=core&format=json)). In a Russian sample of 1609 students aged 17–21, 101 of 1191 young men had deficiencies (8.48%) ([Makarov 2020](https://www.ophthalmojournal.com/opht/article/view/1282)). `[opinion]` For tritanopia only Brettel 1997 is used; for protanopia and deuteranopia Viénot 1999 is slightly better; validation samples are usually fewer than 10 people ([DaltonLens](https://daltonlens.org/opensource-cvd-simulation/)). `[standard; informative]` The 1.4.1 norm sets no threshold. Per Understanding, a difference in both hue and lightness counts as an additional cue at a contrast of 3:1 or more; if the color itself matters, a second cue is needed regardless of contrast ([1.4.1](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/use-of-color.html)). `[inference]` Any ΔE threshold after simulation in a brand book remains opinion. For a red-green pair, the distinction must not rest on hue alone.

**The empirical work confirms the direction but does not promise money.** `[research]` Elliot & Maier warn that the boundaries of the effect must be studied before recommending application ([Annual Review](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/search?query=(%22color%20psychology%22%20AND%20Elliot%20AND%20Maier%20AND%20%22Annual%20Review%22)&resultType=core&format=json&pageSize=3)). Gnambs 2020: 38 samples, 67 effects, a median of 76 people; without correction Δ = −0.13, after correction the effect disappears, power about 18% ([PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7704521/)). O'Donovan 2011, on 327,381 Kuler ratings and 10,743 MTurk themes, did not confirm the wheel templates; lightness and popular adjacent pairs matter, but the themes were studied without context ([PDF](https://www.dgp.toronto.edu/~donovan/color/colorcomp.pdf)). `[inference]` None of the papers read allows promising a lift in conversion or sales from a hue.

### Type: the procedure converges, only the eye judges letterform quality

**Procedure.** `[opinion]` Ruderman starts from the outcome (to calm, to shock, to give comfort), not from trying out typefaces ([interview](https://www.zenbusiness.com/blog/interview-with-ilya-ruderman/)). `[opinion]` First the base, then a partner, and the partner needs a reason: display text, utility text, code, a base that is weak at small sizes ([Schöndorfer](https://pimpmytype.com/?p=6553)).

**Sellers defend custom type, and a custom studio starts by talking clients out of it.** `[opinion]` Christian Schwartz's first move with a client: try to talk them out of it and make sure nothing off the shelf fits (a 2012 interview, a position against his own interest) ([Eye 82](https://eyemagazine.com/feature/article/reputations-commercial-type)). No independent data on the brand effect of a custom typeface was found. `[inference]` A custom typeface is worth discussing if two of three conditions hold: several scripts are needed without a good ready-made solution, licenses get more expensive with scale, typography sits at the center of the identity.

**Technical criteria split into checkable ones and ones judged by eye.** `[vendor]` The GF Guide puts design standards outside its scope and points to checklists; a WARN requires human judgment, and some FAILs apply only to the Google Fonts API ([GF Guide: QA](https://googlefonts.github.io/gf-guide/qa.html)). `[inference]` Automation does not settle outline quality.

### Logo: media requirements are precise, and the link between the mark and money is not shown

**Process.** `[opinion; secondary]` Wheeler describes five stages: Conducting research, Clarifying strategy, Designing identity, Creating touchpoints, Managing assets. The brief is written in the second stage, after research; there is no separate test stage; the number of concepts is not set ([Wheeler's scheme](https://www.logodesignlove.com/brand-identity-process)). `[inference]` An agent needs an order: research, strategy and brief, concepts, applications, approval; testing is added deliberately, the scheme does not guarantee it.

**The typology is not standardized.** `[opinion]` Wheeler has seven types ([table of contents; snippet](https://lib.du.edu.om/bib/23193)), Mollerup three ([Mark Studies](https://markstudies.org/2010/05/25/publication-marks-of-excellence/)). `[inference]` Without direct proof: an unknown brand needs to lean on its name or on descriptiveness, and dropping the name becomes possible once awareness has built up. `[vendor; snippet]` According to Mastercard's own research, more than 80% of people recognize the symbol without the name; the method is not disclosed ([The Drum](https://www.thedrum.com/news/2019/01/07/mastercard-removes-name-circles-logo-act-digital-simplicity)). This precedent must not be carried over to a new brand.

**The Cyrillic wordmark.** No peer-reviewed work on it was found; the typical failures are the same as in the type subsection ([Type Today](https://type.today/en/journal/humanist)). `[inference]` A name in two alphabets should be treated as two wordmarks on one system and drawn in parallel.

**Construction.** `[opinion]` A grid can be real: the G is built on the product icon grid, the circle is corrected against a visual "bite", and the mark is made heavier for small sizes ([Google Design](https://design.google/library/evolving-google-identity)). The golden ratio is confirmed only by after-the-fact analyses (section 6). Optical corrections are documented as practice; there are no numeric norms in what we read. `[research; snippet]` "Simpler is better" is disputed (section 6).

## 2. Rules are written as a number and a ratio: an adjective cannot be checked

`[inference]` What separates a rule from a wish is a measurable condition and a threshold, not how loudly it is worded. What goes into a brand book and why nobody reads it is covered in ["Anatomy of a good brand book"](brand-book-anatomy.md); here we cover only what makes a rule checkable. All pages in this section were read through a summarizer.

**Modality: many have a ladder, almost nobody declares it.** `[standard]` RFC 2119 defines MUST, SHOULD, and MAY, and RFC 8174 keeps the special meaning only for the words in capitals ([RFC 2119](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc2119), [RFC 8174](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8174)). None of the brand documents read declares RFC 2119; this holds for the sample, not for all brands. `[standard]` only for the organization: GOV.UK puts must into a condition on the domain ([Header](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/components/header/)). Russian wording: «запрещается» ("is prohibited"), «не должно быть меньше» ("must not be less than") at the Bank of Russia ([cbr.ru](https://cbr.ru/PSystem/dr/brandbook/)); «должна» ("must"), «рекомендуем» ("we recommend"), «нельзя» ("do not") at Sber ([Sber ID](https://developers.sber.ru/docs/ru/sberid/guidebook)). `[inference]` There is no point copying capitalized MUST; it helps to declare a vocabulary at the start of the document: «обязательно, запрещено, следует, не следует, допускается» ("mandatory, prohibited, should, should not, allowed").

**An exception must be a field of the rule.** The forms vary. `[standard]` only for the organization: written permission ([Yandex](https://yandex.ru/company/rules/logotype)), a condition inside the rule ([GOV.UK](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/components/header/)). `[vendor]` Machine-readable recording: `omitted` with a `reason` field in [DESIGN.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google-labs-code/design.md/main/docs/spec.md), `reportDisables` in [Stylelint](https://stylelint.io/user-guide/configure/).

**A token stores a value, a rule stores a relationship.** `[standard]` DTCG 2025.10 itself states that it is not a W3C Standard: the cover says Final Community Group Report of October 28, 2025, while the status section mentions Candidate Recommendation ([Format](https://www.designtokens.org/tr/2025.10/format/)). `[vendor]` Support in Figma: the sources contradict each other ([W3C CG](https://www.w3.org/community/design-tokens/2026/06/17/thank-you-jina-and-val/), [Atomize; snippet](https://atomize.tools/blog/figma-design-tokens-vibe-coding/)).

`[inference]` The logo as an asset, clear space as a relationship, and tone of voice are not described as a type in [DTCG 2025.10](https://www.designtokens.org/tr/2025.10/format/), [Figma Variables](https://developers.figma.com/docs/rest-api/variables-types/), [Tailwind @theme](https://tailwindcss.com/docs/theme), or [DESIGN.md, alpha](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google-labs-code/design.md/main/docs/spec.md); minimum size in DTCG, Figma, and Tailwind is just a number without meaning, DESIGN.md does not have it, and Do/Don't there remains prose. For Figma and Tailwind this is an inference from the list of types, for DTCG from the part we read. A working setup for an agent: DTCG as the source of values, a build into code and Figma, a separate rules file for what tokens cannot express.

**Rule formats for agents: there is no standard.** `[inference]` Nothing found is a formal standard at the W3C or IETF level. AGENTS.md and Agent Skills are living carriers without brand semantics ([AGENTS.md](https://agents.md/), [Agent Skills](https://agentskills.io/specification)). DESIGN.md remains a single vendor's specification in alpha status ([GitHub](https://github.com/google-labs-code/design.md)). Claims that the file by itself removes generic output run ahead of the data.

`[inference]` Self-generated rules are not a shortcut: a human must write them, or they must pass tests. An agent follows direct instructions fairly well, but its own explanation cannot be trusted, so its report does not count as proof of compliance. **Check schemas show what is missing.** `[standard]` ACT Rules Format 1.1 was published as a W3C Recommendation on February 5, 2026 (1.0: October 2019): applicability, expectations, examples, versions, outcomes `passed`, `failed`, `inapplicable`, `cantTell`, `untested`; the modes `automatic`, `manual`, `semiAuto` are given as an example in the optional Implementations section ([ACT](https://www.w3.org/TR/act-rules-format/)). `[vendor]` In axe-core a rule carries `impact`, tags for the normative criterion, and `incomplete` as a separate outcome ([developer guide](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dequelabs/axe-core/develop/doc/developer-guide.md), [color-contrast.json](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dequelabs/axe-core/develop/lib/rules/color-contrast.json)). `[inference]` None of the schemas reviewed has an escalation field.

## 3. A single table of checks: the machine answers "does it conform", not "is it good"

`[inference]` The agent decides on its own where there is a numeric norm, a reference, and a deterministic tool. If the tool gave no verdict, the value sits near the threshold, the question is legal, or the decision is irreversible, the agent escalates, however confident the model is. Much stays `[auto]` only on structured input: if the agent delivers a raster, the same rules drop to `[semi-auto]`.

**How to read the table.** Table caption: only the `[test]` marker is labeled. Treat links to WCAG and OpenType as `[standard]`, tool documentation and MetricGate as `[vendor]`, and O'Donovan, Li, Duan, Edwards as `[research]`; the labels are refined in sections 1–3. "Our threshold" means the number was proposed by the researchers and is not validated: the ±0.1 zone around the contrast threshold, 3:1 for small sizes, ΔE for a token, 2–3 rounds of critique, a panel of 3–7 people, the targets for the base logo versions. Tools without `[test]` are described from their documentation and were not run locally.

### Mandatory escalation triggers

`[inference]` Our own list, with the grounds given:

1. The tool gave no verdict: `incomplete`, a render error, a skip ([axe API](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dequelabs/axe-core/develop/doc/API.md)).
2. The value is in a predefined zone near the threshold (our threshold), or two implementations or algorithms disagree (`[test]` coloraide, DaltonLens, APCA).
3. A model's claim about pixels, colors, strokes, or the font is not confirmed by a measurement ([BlindTest](https://vlmsareblind.github.io), [Biased](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.23941), [ColorBench](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.10514v3)).
4. The model disagrees with itself across repeats or rephrasings ([MM-R³](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-acl.246/)).
5. The question is legal: a font license, a trademark ([OpenType name](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/typography/opentype/spec/name)).
6. The decision is external or hard to reverse: the final mark, a change to the brand book, publication, updating a reference.
7. The question is about meaning, character, and fit with the strategy ([Vessel](https://idw-online.de/en/news700073)).
8. The model has gone through several rounds and the critique keeps going ([Duan CHI 2024](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.13139v1)).

**A human alone is unreliable too.** `[research]` Fleiss κ when rating design feedback is 0.112 and 0.100 ([Duan CHI](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.13139v1)); for engineering sketches κ is 0.26 for creativity and 0.59 for usefulness ([Edwards; preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2504.00938)). `[test; calculation]` By the Spearman–Brown formula, with a single-rater ICC of .26, .33, and .54, a reliability of .70 needs 7, 5, and 2 raters; this is an order of magnitude from engineering sketches, not a norm. No data on expert reliability specifically for logos was found. `[inference]` Criteria first, ratings independently, discussion afterward; craft to the experts, perception to the audience, and do not mix them into one score.

### Prohibitions related to generative AI

`[vendor]` The [Commercial Type EULA](https://commercialtype.com/licensing) prohibits using the font with generative and other AI (cl. 2(b)(iv)); the vendor's explanation applies the ban to training on the font's designs and data, and places outside it a mention in a style guide and CSS in AI-generated HTML. The explanation does not replace the contract; confirm a disputed scenario in writing. In the [type.today EULA](https://type.today/ru/license) a search for words about artificial intelligence and neural networks found nothing (per the report author's snapshot, see "Could not verify"); `[standard; via court]` the absence of a ban does not count as consent: «Отсутствие запрета не считается согласием (разрешением)» ("The absence of a prohibition is not considered consent (permission)") (para. 1 of Art. 1229 of the Civil Code, as quoted by the [IP Court](https://www.garant.ru/products/ipo/prime/doc/413910707/)). `[standard]` Per the OFL FAQ, a font produced by a system trained on OFL fonts counts as derivative and stays under OFL (1.25), and AI design tools are allowed if the result is not a font (1.26) ([FAQ](https://openfontlicense.org/ofl-faq/)). `[inference]` If a training set is built on commercial fonts, the EULAs are checked separately. `[vendor]` The Swiss Typefaces EULA does not mention AI; the notes do not cover the other foundries on this question.

### Russian law: a typeface is protected as a work of graphic art

`[inference]` Courts consistently recognize a typeface as an object of copyright, although the Civil Code does not name it. Disputes are about proof of rights, about whether letters match, and about the amount of compensation. The brand is liable to the rights holder and can then recover from the contractor. `[opinion]` Registering a trademark does not cure the defect (an ISP Legal recommendation, not a court finding). No case law applying OFL in Russian courts was found. The Civil Code articles were read in GARANT and on mirrors, with no cross-check against the official portal.

| Provision `[standard]` | Content |
|---|---|
| [Art. 1259](https://www.zakonrf.info/gk/1259/) | Works regardless of merit; the list includes graphic works and design, typefaces are not named. Para. 7: a part of a work is protected if it is an independent result of creative work |
| [Art. 1235](https://gkodeksrf.ru/ch-4/rzd-7/gl-69/st-1235-gk-rf) | Written form; a right not stated is not considered granted; the default territory is the Russian Federation; the default term is 5 years. Read only on gkodeksrf.ru, where Art. 1301 is out of date; the version needs checking |

| [Art. 1301](https://base.garant.ru/10164072/33baf11fff1f64e732fcb2ef0678c18a/) | Compensation: from 10 thousand to 10 million rubles, or double the value of the counterfeit copies, or double the value of the right of use (para. 1). ### What a script can say about a license, and what to keep

`[inference]` A script (fontTools: name 0, 7, 13, 14, `fsType`, `getBestCmap()`) answers what the file declares and which characters it contains. It cannot answer whether the company has the right. The system cannot tell a genuine file from a modified one, and `fsType` describes only embedding in documents.

`[standard]` The [name specification](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/typography/opentype/spec/name) calls ID 13 the license description and advises a legal review of its content. `[court]` In А56-49280/2025 the dispute was about the seller's own chain of title, so even the label of a genuine seller can be challenged ([ruling](https://www.garant.ru/products/ipo/prime/doc/413910707/)).

`[inference]` The order for an agent: identify the source, get the license file, cross-check METADATA and name, check the RFN and the manner of use, check cmap and Hyperglot, request documents. Any gap gives the status "needs confirmation", not "allowed". The archive contents are our own proposal; keep it for the whole period of use plus at least 3 years, because the court counts the limitation period from when the rights holder learned of the infringement ([RAPSI; snippet](https://rapsinews.ru/judicial_news/20201113/306495042.html)).

| Commercial font | The invoice with the licensee, styles, type, and limits; a dated EULA snapshot; the payment document; the logo license and permission to register the trademark, if required ([Contrast](https://contrastfoundry.com/licensing)); monthly view statistics; an installation register |
| Contractor | A list of third-party materials, a warranty, confirmation of licenses; dated snapshots of the website and layouts |

A logo in Latin script without registration and without a Russian duplicate in a consumer setting is in the risk zone. For B2B SaaS, direct application is not obvious, but a fashion-retail client selling to consumers falls under the provision. Needs a patent attorney or a lawyer.

### Similarity criteria: overall impression, not counting features

`[standard]` [Rules No. 482](https://rospatent.gov.ru/ru/documents/482-prikaz-minekonomrazvitiya-rossii-ot-20-07-2015-482): similarity exists if a designation is associated with another as a whole despite individual differences (para. 41). Sound: closeness of sounds, syllables, stress, one designation contained in the other. Graphics: overall impression, typeface, character of the letters, **alphabet**, color. Meaning: similarity of concepts, **the same meaning in different languages**. Features are considered both separately and in combination (para. 42, [СИП-645/2018](https://www.garant.ru/products/ipo/prime/doc/72049748/)). Relatedness is determined by whether consumers could in principle get the impression of a single manufacturer (para. 45, [С01-1782/2022](https://www.garant.ru/products/ipo/prime/doc/408141951/)). Under para. 162 of Plenum Resolution No. 10, the court establishes similarity by inner conviction, taking strong and weak elements into account (same source). Logos (para. 43): outer form, symmetry, meaning, type and character of the image, combination of colors and tones. Combined marks (para. 44): the features of paras. 42 and 43 and the importance of where the similar element sits. `[standard]` Art. 1483 prohibits designations lacking distinctiveness (para. 1), false ones (para. 3), ones similar to earlier filed and protected marks for related goods (para. 6), to company names and commercial designations for related goods with earlier rights (para. 8), and to well-known works, characters, and names (para. 9) ([GARANT](https://base.garant.ru/10164072/de367e1761fc8dd5de9de615d6480300/)).

Translation cannot be ignored. Weak graphics on top of a similar word do not save a logo.

## 7. What this means for training agents

The whole section carries the `[inference]` label: the grounds are named, and these recommendations have no causal test.

**Teach splitting the question, not judging beauty.** The agent divides the task into a measurable part and a remainder and reports what checked each part. Passing every `[auto]` check does not mean the mark is good. The report separates "measured: tool, version, command, value" from "the model's opinion". The micro level (kerning, a 2 px shift, hex, stroke counts) is fed to the model as measurements from the vector, DOM, or pixels: it will not reliably see these from a raster ([Haraguchi](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.08885), [ColorBench](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.10514v3)).

**The format of a checkable rule.** We consider six fields (condition, requirement, threshold, check method, action on failure, escalation) a working construction, not a standard. Following [ACT](https://www.w3.org/TR/act-rules-format/) and [axe-core](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dequelabs/axe-core/develop/doc/developer-guide.md), we added a requirement level, a third outcome, a version, examples, an exception expiry, and a source link. The values in the card are illustrative; the threshold comes from [Spotify](https://developer.spotify.com/documentation/design).

```yaml
id: logo.clearspace.primary
version: 1.2.0
source: "Spotify Design Guidelines, Logo"
level: must            # from the document's modality vocabulary
type_of_basis: standard_for_brand   # standard, consensus, opinion: a label from this report
applies_when: "full logo in DOM, SVG, or a Figma node"
requirement: "distance from the mark's bbox to any neighboring bbox is at least the threshold"
threshold: "0.5 * icon_height"
threshold_validated: false
check:
  mode: automatic      # automatic | semi_auto | manual, following the ACT example
  method: "bbox from a structured layout"
outcomes: [passed, failed, inapplicable, cant_tell]   # cant_tell: a raster without layers
on_fail: "block export, suggest a shift"
on_cant_tell: "hand to a human, do not count as passed"
exceptions:
  - id: ex-001
    reason: "example exception with a reason and an expiry"
    expires: 2027-01-01
owner: "brand team"
escalate_to: "lead brand designer"
examples: {pass: [good-1.svg], fail: [bad-1.svg]}
```

The `applies_when` field separates "not applicable" from "violated". The `cant_tell` status must not be equated with success, or the agent gets an incentive to pick the format that is harder to check. Rules that cannot be formalized ("we joke, but not inappropriately", "reflects the brand's character", "do not create the impression of a partnership") are taught through examples and handed to a human with a clear question; a proxy metric here would reward copying.

**How to write rules.** A human writes them, or the rules pass tests: self-generated descriptions give no gain ([ETH](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.11988), [SkillsBench](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.12670v1)). Keep them short and with a working example: 1–3 compact skills beat comprehensive documentation.

**Working sequences.**

| Task | Order |
|---|---|
| Palette | The brand color and its role from a human; the scale from the system in a color-preserving mode; recompute all pairs in every theme by the WCAG formula; CVD as candidates; values for screen, paper, and fabric separately; approval by a human in context |
| Type | Goal and environment; languages and characters; the `[auto]` filter; the license from the file and the manner of use; one family; a second only with a stated reason; a test in Russian with Л and П, З and 3, ₽ and №; a human's decision |
| Logo | Research, strategy, and brief; concepts; a vector master from a human, pinned by hash; a run across the media; a panel of people; a preliminary check of the mark and the chain of title before investing in advertising and packaging |
| Name | The checklist in section 5; the outcome is only "risk candidates found" or "stop" |

**What the agent decides on its own, where there is a norm, a reference, and a deterministic tool.**

| Decides on its own | Condition |
|---|---|
| Text contrast on a flat background | The value is outside the zone near the threshold (our threshold) |
| Character coverage, tofu, checking ready-made subsets | Everything is found; if a character is missing, stop; creating a subset of a file with an RFN: stop |
| Presence of files, sizes, formats, safe zone | Per the specification of the specific medium |
| SVG validity, no text or raster, geometry after optimization | The diff is empty at the reference sizes |
| Conformance to tokens and exact values | Exceptions are not changed |
| Typographic lint | Per the rules of the brand guide |
| Spelling variants, a local index of candidates | The output is only "candidates"; a human confirms the variants are complete |

The agent may do the first pass of critique on its own, but this is `[semi-auto]`: every remark is confirmed by a measurement or shown to a human; no more than 2–3 rounds (our threshold).

**Where it must stop.** The triggers in section 3 and the stop conditions in section 5 apply regardless of the model's confidence. In addition:

| Stop | Why |
|---|---|
| A license for a logo, a product, web embedding; a corporate font; modifying a file with an RFN | A record in the file is not a contract ([Art. 1235](https://gkodeksrf.ru/ch-4/rzd-7/gl-69/st-1235-gk-rf)) |
| Any candidate with a matching strong element in close classes, a rights dispute, an incomplete search | A court's conclusion does not reduce to a metric ([С01-1782/2022](https://www.garant.ru/products/ipo/prime/doc/408141951/)) |
| A claim about the effect on money, conversion, recognition | There is almost no causal data; with too little traffic, do not claim an effect ([Kohavi](https://exp-platform.com/Documents/2014%20experimentersRulesOfThumb.pdf)) |
| An LLM panel instead of people in the final decision | There is no stable agreement with people ([Tsai & Lai; preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.25677)) |
| A letter "from Rospatent", a claim letter, a marketplace complaint | Verify through official channels ([Rospatent](https://rospatent.gov.ru/ru/news/05-11-2026-vnimanie-moshenniki-otpravlyayut-predpisaniya-ot-imeni-rospatenta)) |
