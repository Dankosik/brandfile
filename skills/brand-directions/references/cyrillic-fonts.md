# Cyrillic Font Notes

Data from 2026-11-06. License and quality are separate checks: verify the file you will actually use.

## Test line

On each direction, set Л beside П and З beside 3, plus Ж К Ф Я Ч Ю Ы Щ ё № ₽ « » —, in roman and italic, at every weight it uses.

**Usable with care:** Rubik and Montserrat (headings, some flaws), Source Serif 4 (acceptable), Cormorant (display, inconsistent weights), Geologica and Playfair Display (flawed), EB Garamond (mediocre).

**Experts advise against:** Inter (errors at extreme weights), Manrope (concept and execution), Jost, Source Sans 3, Open Sans (especially extreme weights), Roboto (safer at medium weights). Noto Serif was rated badly in 2021, before Noto was updated, so look again.

**No independent rating found:** Onest, Unbounded, PT Mono, IBM Plex Mono, Roboto Serif, TikTok Sans.

The letters of an unmodified open font in a logo stay available to competitors.

## OFL 1.1

- **Logos:** allowed, with no fee or credit, by the license author's FAQ. No Russian court practice on OFL turned up, so a logo meant to last decades is worth a lawyer's look.
- **Subsetting is a modification.** With RFN (PT, IBM Plex, Source, Lora, Playfair Display), a modified file must be renamed or have written permission, so self-host the full file.
- **Mobile apps:** ship the copyright notice and the license text.
- **The source file decides:** Source Serif 4 has no RFN in the google/fonts copy, but Adobe's license declares one.

A Russian site needs more than the `cyrillic` range: in Google Fonts' split, spaces, digits, « » and — sit in `latin`, and ₽ in `latin-ext`.

## Commercial fonts

A desktop license covers a logo only where its text says so.

| Foundry | Logo |
|---|---|
| type.today | Included in the desktop license, by its FAQ |
| Swiss Typefaces | Included, with conditions |
| Contrast Foundry | Separate license |
| TypeType | Separate Logo license |
| Brownfox | Separate Logotype license |
| Paratype | Separate license, for fonts under its own license |
| Fontfabric | Desktop covers static logos; trademark not described |

Also check pageview limits, one license per domain, server and app use, video and social use, and handing files to a contractor. A designer may pass on finished images but not the font files, and some foundries require the client's own license even for outlined work.

Russian courts treat a typeface as a protected work. Since 4 January 2026 compensation is 10,000 to 10 million rubles per violation, and registering a trademark does not cure it.

## Fonts that look free but are not

Yandex Sans, YS Text, VK Sans, MTS Sans, SB Sans: closed without written permission. Gilroy: the terms of the two free weights are unconfirmed, and full sets on other sites are unlicensed. Arial and Times New Roman: shipping with an operating system grants no web or app rights. With no visible license, pick an open font with a similar feel.

For each chosen font, keep the source URL, the license text, and the date. Any gap means "needs confirmation".
