# Russian Notes

Record each of these choices in `{#voice}`, or in `{#start}` when it applies to every text.

- **Address.** "вы" unless the brief chose "ты". Lowercase "вы" on the site and in mailings to many people, capital "Вы" only in a letter to one person if the author wants it. For a message to one signed-in user, record the brief's choice or list it in `{#open}`. A button in the infinitive needs no choice.
- **Gender.** Where "ты" or "я" would show gender in the past tense, write the present tense or an impersonal form. Record who says "я", who says "мы", and the form of each speaker, such as an impersonal system voice.
- **English rules.** Contractions, singular they, sentence-length limits, and bans on "please" do not carry over. Write the brand's own rule instead, measured on their good texts where they have some.
- **Инфостиль** is clarity, not a voice. Its stop-word lists are an editor's hints, so keep them out of the book's rules.
- **The name in templates.** "сервис Корт" does not decline, "Корт" alone does, and declension can have legal weight. Record one form, and check it in the templates, not only in finished texts.
- **Typography.** Record the house rules: «ёлочки» with „лапки“ inside; `—` with spaces, the one before it non-breaking; ranges without spaces (1914–1918); non-breaking spaces after short prepositions and with № § and units; ё everywhere or only where needed, since it touches search and the logo; ₽ after the sum with a space. Call them conventions, not "по ГОСТу".
